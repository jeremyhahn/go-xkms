WebAuthn: the qrdb user store refuses a user handle that belongs to another tenant. `GetByID` reads users through the service binding, which sees every tenant, and now answers a handle outside the store's tenant as it answers an unknown handle (`ErrUserNotFound`), so a passkey registered in one tenant no longer completes a handle-based or discoverable login at another tenant's relying party. `Delete` treats such a handle as absent in its tenant the same way (#1873).
