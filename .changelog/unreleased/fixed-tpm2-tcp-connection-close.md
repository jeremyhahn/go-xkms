- **TPM 2.0: closing a TPM reached over TCP or a Unix socket releases the connection.** `TPM2.Close` closed device files and the simulator but never the network transport `NewTPM2` opened for a `CommandAddress` (swtpm) or a `.sock` device, so a long-running process held its connection until it exited -- and swtpm, which serves one client at a time, blocked every other client meanwhile (for about two minutes per call in the vault integration suite). `NewTPM2` also closes the connection when it fails after dialling.
