- **`ek create` after a TPM clear no longer refuses with "carry no creation template".** A successful TPM2_Clear (TCG TPM 2.0 Part 3 §24.6) through `Clear`, `ClearForReprovision` (LOCKOUT or PLATFORM) or `FactoryResetWithClear` now drops every cached Owner and Endorsement hierarchy value in `pkg/tpm2`: the EK, IAK, IDevID, LAK, SSRK and Platform SRK attributes and the EK public keys. `EKAttributes()` re-derives the EK from the config, with its TCG default creation template, instead of returning the pre-clear value. `FactoryReset` drops the same set, the EK values included. A refused clear leaves the cache as it was (#1667).
