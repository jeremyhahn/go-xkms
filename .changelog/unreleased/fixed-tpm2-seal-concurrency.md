- **`TPM2.Seal` and `TPM2.Unseal` are safe to call from many goroutines on one TPM.** Each is a sequence of commands over sessions and transient objects the TPM holds between them, and concurrent callers interleaved those sequences: the TPM refused them with `TPM_RC_BAD_AUTH`, `TPM_RC_OBJECT_MEMORY` or `TPM_RC_VALUE`, so an enclave provider shared across goroutines could not protect a key. A per-TPM mutex now holds each Seal and each Unseal for its whole sequence.
