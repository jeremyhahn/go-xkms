- **OIDC tokens persist through the injected DAO factory** -- `oidc.NewDAOStore` takes a `*qrdbsdk.DAOFactory` instead of a `KVStore` and refuses a nil factory, or one that does not serve the `oidc` database, with `ErrDAOCreation`; `ErrNilBackend` is gone. `MemoryTokenStore` (and the SDK's `NewMemoryTokenStore`) is deleted. New `dao.NewStoreFactory` (SDK `NewStoreDAOFactory`) serves one `KVStore` as the factory for every database, for hosts with no go-qrdb node: xkey's GUI builds its OIDC token store with it over the barrier backend, or over a memory store when there is no barrier. Tokens stored before this change are not migrated.
