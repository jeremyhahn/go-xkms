- **The PIV auth factor's `Verify` now accepts the RSA and Ed25519 enrollments its own `Enroll` can produce, not just ECDSA** (#1289). `Enroll` has always offered `rsa2048`, `rsa4096` and `ed25519` alongside `ecdsap256`/`ecdsap384`, but `Verify` type-asserted the enrolled key to `*ecdsa.PublicKey` and rejected everything else, so an RSA- or Ed25519-enrolled custodian could never complete a challenge. `Verify` now dispatches on the enrolled key's algorithm: ECDSA stays ASN.1 DER `(r, s)` over SHA-256(nonce); RSA verifies PKCS#1 v1.5 over SHA-256(nonce) and refuses moduli under 2048 bits (`types.ValidateRSAKeySize`); Ed25519 verifies the raw signature over the raw nonce, since Ed25519 hashes its own input and does not accept a pre-hashed digest for standard signing. `SignNonce`, the same package's signature producer, matches: it now signs the raw nonce for Ed25519 rather than a SHA-256 digest Go's `ed25519.Sign` would refuse.
  - Files: `pkg/factor/piv/factor.go`.
  - Verified by `TestPIVFactor_Verify_MultiAlgorithm` (ECDSA P-256, RSA-2048, Ed25519 — genuine signature, wrong signature, key-type mismatch, and a different key of the same type) and `TestPIVFactor_Verify_RejectsUndersizedRSAKey`.
