- **Continuous attestation verdicts on the QUIC and REST clients and sdk/go.** A new `wire.AttestationVerdictClient` calls the REST verdict routes `GET /api/v1/attestation/verdicts?device=`, `GET /api/v1/attestation/verdicts/latest?device=` and `POST /api/v1/attestation/verdicts`. The QUIC and REST client transports serve `ListAttestationVerdicts`, `GetLatestAttestationVerdict` and `ReattestDevice` through it and answer not-connected before they connect. sdk/go/transport re-exports `AttestationVerdictService`, `AttestationVerdictRequest`, `AttestationVerdict` and `ListAttestationVerdictsResponse`.
