- **A vTPM is ready only once it answers a command** -- `vtpm.Manager.Start` (and a supervised restart) treated a vTPM as ready as soon as its unix socket accepted a connection, but swtpm binds the socket before it decrypts and loads its state. On a loaded host, a vTPM started over state encrypted under another key was reported started and only then exited. Readiness is now a TPM2_GetRandom round trip, which swtpm answers only after its state has loaded. Files: `pkg/tpm2/vtpm/manager.go`.
