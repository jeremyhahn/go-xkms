- **go-qrdb v0.25.0-alpha.** Every module moves to go-qrdb and its `sdk/go` v0.25.0-alpha, go-quicraft v0.15.0-alpha and go-enclave v0.6.1-alpha. Consumer-visible changes: `sdk/go/secure` drops the memguard-era `CatchInterrupt`, `EnablePurge`, `Purge`, `PurgeOrPanic` and `NotInitializedError` for `WipeAll` (a binary defers it at startup; xkmsd's teardown is now the single `enclave.WipeAll`); `types.NewLiteralSecretValue` returns `(*SecretValue, error)`, failing when the value cannot be sealed; `xkmsctl cluster add` takes `--identity` (the replica's `spki:` Raft member identity); the `sdk/go/dek` re-export and `QRDBTenantKeyManager` alias are removed, as go-qrdb removed what they aliased.
