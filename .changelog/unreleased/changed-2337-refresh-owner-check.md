- **A refresh token is exchanged only for the record saved for its own subject and tenant.** `Issuer.Refresh` compares the stored refresh record's subject and tenant with the token's `sub` and `tenant_id` claims and refuses a mismatch with the generic revoked error, before any reuse handling, so nothing is minted or rotated. Records now store the canonical tenant (a system-tenant session names the system tenant), and a record saved without a tenant (before the tenant field existed) is refused at refresh, so those sessions end within one refresh lifetime and their users sign in again.
