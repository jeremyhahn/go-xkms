- **Password-vault passkeys are created for the server's WebAuthn relying party, and the web console enrolls and unlocks with them.** The fixed relying party `xkms-password-vault` is gone: a browser refuses a non-domain ID, so a passkey from the CLI could never open in the console. The vault status now advertises `passkey_rp_id` (the configured `webauthn.rp_id`; a host module passes `WithServiceModulePasswordVaultRelyingParty` or its WebAuthn config), every passkey factor records the `rp_id` it was created for, and every client -- `pkg/vaultpasskey` (xkmsctl, the xkey CLI and GUI) and the new `sdk/ts` `enrollVaultPasskey`/`unlockVaultPasskey` (WebAuthn PRF, `userVerification: "required"`, `prf.evalByCredential`) -- creates for the advertised ID and evaluates with the recorded one. With no relying party configured, passkey enrollment answers `ErrVaultPasskeyUnavailable` (REST 412, gRPC FailedPrecondition); another relying party is `ErrVaultRelyingPartyMismatch` (400). A browser or key without PRF is the typed `VaultPasskeyPrfUnsupportedError`, never a fallback. The console creates a vault with a passkey, unlocks with one, recovers with a new one and adds one from *Vault factors*. Cross-surface secret vectors (`pkg/fido2/testdata/vault_passkey_secret_vectors.json`) are checked by `pkg/vaultpasskey` and `sdk/ts`; a Playwright spec runs enroll and unlock through Chrome's WebAuthn stack with a CDP virtual authenticator with PRF. **No migration:** passkeys enrolled for `xkms-password-vault` are not carried forward (the burn-in node is being rebuilt).
