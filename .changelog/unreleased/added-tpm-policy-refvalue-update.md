- **A TPM policy branch can be approved for an update before it boots, from signed reference values.** `tpm policy update <name> --branch <n> --reference-values <corim>` (REST `{branch, reference_values}`, gRPC, MCP, xkmsctl, xkey CLI and GUI) verifies a COSE-signed CoRIM against the new `backends.tpm2.reference_signer` (`public_key` or `roots`), predicts the branch's PCR values by replaying the boot event log with the CoRIM's component digests (or takes its final values), re-approves the branch and records the CoRIM's id, signer and digest, which `verify` reports. An unsigned, foreign or tampered CoRIM, a missing branch or pcr condition, or a node without a reference signer is refused and leaves the approvals unchanged; a live re-capture drops the reference. The permission is `tpm_policy:update`. On the TPM simulator the registered image unseals and an unregistered one stays refused (`TestService_UpdateBranchFromReferenceValues`, `TestService_UpdateBranchRefusals`).
