- **xkmsd serves PCR policies (`/api/v1/policies/*`) when it has a TPM.** xkmsd built no PCR reader, so its policy manager was always off and every policy route answered "not configured". With `backends.tpm2.enabled`, xkmsd now builds the policy manager through `compose.BuildPolicyManager` -- the path an embedding host's `WithServiceModulePCRReader` takes -- over a new `tpm2.BankPCRReader`, which reads only the named bank through the TPM2 backend's existing TPM open (no second open of the device). Without a TPM, policies stay off and xkmsd logs `xkms feature off feature=policies` with the typed `compose.FeatureDisabledError` reason. `BankPCRReader.ReadPCRs` refuses an unknown bank (`ErrInvalidPCRBankType`) and an index outside 0..23 (`ErrInvalidPCRIndex`) before touching the TPM, and reports an unreadable PCR, including one in a bank the TPM has not allocated (`ErrPCRBankUnavailable`), as a `*tpm2.PCRReadError`.
  - Files: `pkg/tpm2/pcr_reader.go`, `pkg/server/backend_tpm2.go`, `pkg/server/server.go`, `docs/sealing-and-barrier/api.md`, `docs/host-embedding/architecture.md`.
  - Verified by `TestBankPCRReader_ReadsTheNamedBank` and `TestBankPCRReader_RefusesBeforeTouchingTheTPM` (`pkg/tpm2`), and `TestInitializePolicyManager_TPMIsThePCRReader` (`pkg/server`, a simulator-backed xkmsd creating and verifying a policy through the XKMSService) and `TestInitializePolicyManager_NoTPMLeavesPoliciesOff`.
