- **Server-side OIDC credentials: `pkg/oidc/credential`, a service that holds named OAuth/OIDC logins on the tenant barrier and keeps them fresh.** A credential is tenant-shared or personal (`pkg/scope`: `{tenant}/oidc/shared/{name}`, `{tenant}/oidc/users/{subject}/{name}`); a personal credential is visible to its owner only, never to the system tenant. A login arrives by a device flow the server runs (`StartDeviceLogin`/`PollDeviceLogin`) or by importing tokens (`Create` with tokens, `ImportTokens`). `GetAccessToken` returns a current access token by reference, refreshing first inside the refresh margin (default five minutes) and still serving a valid token when the provider is briefly down. A rotating refresh token is redeemed under a per-credential lock -- an in-process mutex plus, on a raft cluster, a qrdb lease -- and persisted with the barrier's compare-and-swap, so concurrent consumers cause exactly one refresh. A rejected refresh (`invalid_grant`, `refresh_token_reused`, ...) moves the credential to `relogin_required` and emits `oidc_credential.relogin_required` (subscribers and the audit log); a non-refreshable token (the `anthropic` template) is tracked to its expiry and moves to `expired`. A scheduler started with `Start` refreshes every due credential in every unsealed tenant, on the node holding the `xkms/oidc/scheduler` qrdb lease; a standalone node, whose qrdb refuses the raft-only lease family, is its own leader.
