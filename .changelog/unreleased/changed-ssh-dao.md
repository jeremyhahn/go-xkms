- **SSH keys, known_hosts pins, authorized_keys entries, connection profiles and console sessions are go-qrdb DAO entities** -- every `pkg/ssh` store persists through a `*qrdbsdk.DAOFactory` serving the `ssh` database: `keys.NewDAOStore(daos)`, `known_hosts.NewDAOStore(daos)` and `authorized.NewDAOStore(daos)` (each with `Partition(tenantID)`), `profile.NewStore(daos)` and `sessions.NewStore(daos)`, instead of a `storage.Backend`. The SDK follows: `sdkssh.NewStores(daos)` feeds `EmbeddedOptions{Stores}`, `NewProfileStore`, `NewHostKeyStore` and `NewConsoleSessionStore` take the DAO factory, and `tenancy.Options{Storage}` is now `tenancy.Options{DAOs}` (`Close` no longer closes it). `compose.BuildSSHClient` takes the DAO factory and `compose.Databases` always includes `ssh`. The `BackendStore` types, `known_hosts.LegacyPinError`, `keys.SerializationError`, `authorized.SerializationError` and `authorized.UnsupportedVersionError` are gone. Records written by the old storage-backed stores are not read.
