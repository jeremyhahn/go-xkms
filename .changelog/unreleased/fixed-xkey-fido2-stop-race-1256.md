- **The xkey GUI's FIDO2 virtual device service no longer panics under concurrent Stop calls** (#1256). Wails dispatches bound method calls on separate goroutines, so a toggle in the UI could race app shutdown, or race itself: two callers could both observe the "running" check favorably, and both proceed to `close(stopCh)`, panicking on the second close. `cleanup()` also cleared `device`/`uhidDev` without synchronization, and `device.Close()` ran once in `Stop` and again, redundantly, in `cleanup()`. `Start` had the mirror problem: two concurrent calls could both pass the "already running" check and race to install a device and UHID handle. `Start` and `Stop` now serialize on a dedicated mutex for their full duration and gate the running state with `atomic.Bool.CompareAndSwap`, so exactly one caller wins each transition; `Stop` clears `device` immediately after closing it so `cleanup`'s nil check never closes it a second time.
  - Files: `xkey/pkg/gui/services/fido2_device_service.go`.
  - Verified by `TestFIDO2DeviceService_Stop_ConcurrentCalls_ExactlyOneSucceeds`, `TestFIDO2DeviceService_Stop_ClosesDeviceExactlyOnce`, `TestFIDO2DeviceService_Stop_ConcurrentCalls_ClosesDeviceExactlyOnce`, and `TestFIDO2DeviceService_Start_ConcurrentCalls_WhenRunning_AllFail`, all under `-race`.
