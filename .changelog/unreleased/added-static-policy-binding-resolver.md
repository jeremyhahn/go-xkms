- **The static TPM policy resolver refuses unsigned approvals, and the SDK classifies its refusals.** `NewTPMStaticPolicyResolver` refuses a binding record whose approval carries no signature (`ErrTPMPolicyApprovalInvalid`), and sdk/go now exports `TPMPolicyBindingFormatError`, `ErrTPMPolicyApprovalInvalid` and `ErrTPMPolicyApprovalMismatch`, so a consumer such as an initrd tells a corrupt, unsigned or foreign record apart without importing `pkg/*`. A key is usable with only its `.tpmpolicy` record, and a tampered signature, a record re-signed by a foreign authority, stripped approvals and an unknown policy authorize nothing on the TPM simulator (`TestStaticPolicyResolver_AuthorizesFromTheRecord`, `TestStaticPolicyResolver_Refusals`, `TestNewTPMStaticPolicyResolver_Refusals`).
