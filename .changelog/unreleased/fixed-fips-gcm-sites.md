- **Running under `GODEBUG=fips140=only` no longer crashes or seals with a refused GCM.** xkmsctl FROST dealer packages are sealed as JWE `ECDH-ES+A256KW` / `A256CBC-HS512` (RFC 7518 §5.2.5) rather than `A256GCM`, because go-jose seals GCM under its own arbitrary IV, which the FIPS module refuses; packages sealed with `A256GCM` no longer import, so re-export them. The personal-vault HKDF (`pkg/staticpw`) uses stdlib `crypto/hkdf`, so a secret under 112 bits, such as a mistyped recovery code, is a `*VaultCryptoError` instead of a panic. Tests that exercise X25519 (Noise NK in `pkg/trust/bootstrap`, `pkg/pairingproto`, `pkg/trust/clusterca`, `xkmsctl bootstrap noise`) or arbitrary-IV GCM (vault BYOK, Azure Key Vault nonces) assert the stdlib refusal under fips140=only and the round trip outside it, through the new `fipstest.OutsideFIPSAfterRefusal`. See `docs/fips/gcm-sites.md` (part of #23).
