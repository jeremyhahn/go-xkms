- **`xkmsctl attestation endorsement put|list|delete` manage the Verifier's endorsement store.** `put <name> --kind ak-root|reference-signer --cert <pem>` (or `--public-key <pem>`, exactly one) loads an AK root or a reference-value signer, `list [--kind]` shows the tenant's endorsements (`-o json` prints the response) and `delete <name>` removes one. Each calls the sdk/go `Client` verb of the same name; xkmsd validates the PEM (go-xkms#1660).
