- **An ACME account can no longer open the server-wide `http01` circuit breaker by naming refused challenge targets** (#1181). Every `http-01` failure counted toward the one `http01` breaker, including challenges the validation client refused without contacting anything -- an invalid target, or one resolving to a loopback, private or metadata address. Five such challenges (the default `max_failures`) opened the breaker and failed every account's `http-01` validation for the reset timeout.
  - New `validation.IsTargetRejected(err)` reports a `*validation.Error` of `KindInvalidTarget` or `KindTargetRefused`.
  - `circuitbreaker.WrapChallengeVerifier` runs the verifier with that predicate as an exclusion: an excluded error is returned to the caller but recorded as neither failure nor success (so it cannot reset the failure count either), and a half-open slot it held is released. Connection failures to contacted targets, the breaker timeout, bad status, redirects, oversized responses and key-authorization mismatches still count.
  - The breaker remains global rather than per target host; a per-host breaker needs bounded, evicting breaker storage and a per-host health view, and is left as a follow-up.
  - Files: `pkg/acme/challenges/validation/errors.go`, `pkg/acme/server/circuitbreaker/{circuitbreaker,decorators}.go`, `docs/acme-issuance/{architecture,engineering,user}.md`.
  - Verified by `pkg/acme/server/circuitbreaker/decorators_test.go` (fifty refused/invalid targets through a real `validation.Client` leave a `MaxFailures: 1` breaker closed; connection failures to a closed loopback port open it; interleaved refusals do not hold it closed), `pkg/acme/server/circuitbreaker/exclusion_test.go` and `pkg/acme/challenges/validation/errors_test.go`.
