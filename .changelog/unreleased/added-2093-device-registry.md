- **Backend mode has a paired-device registry.** go-xkms kept no record of the devices a tenant paired, so it could neither verify nor revoke one. The new library `pkg/devicereg` stores one record per tenant and device ID on the qrdb DAO: `Pair` refuses a duplicate ID, `Revoke` is idempotent and keeps the record, `List` is ordered by ID, and a tenant can neither read nor revoke another tenant's device. No permission, route or service verb yet (row device-share-policy). See `docs/device-registry/architecture.md`.
