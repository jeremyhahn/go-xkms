- **PIV login checks the account's `piv` permitted method, not `mtls`** -- `POST /api/v1/auth/login/piv` enforced the qrdb permitted-method policy with `mtls` while recording the session as `piv`, so an account permitted only mTLS API access could mint a session with a smart card, and a `piv`-only account was refused. The exchange now checks and records go-qrdb's `MethodPIV`. Accounts that should keep PIV login need `piv` in their `auth_methods`. File: `pkg/api/rest/handlers_auth.go`.
