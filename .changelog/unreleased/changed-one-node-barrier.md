- **xkmsd has one barrier: the embedded qrdb node's** -- every hardware and cloud seal strategy (`tpm2`, `pkcs11`, `awskms`, `gcpkms`, `azurekv`, `vault`) is now injected into the node's own barrier through `node.WithSealStrategy` and `sdk.WithStrategyRegistry` (`embeddednode.NodeSealOptions`), and xkmsd adopts the node's registry and tenant service. The second, xkms-built barrier (`initializeBarrier`, `initializeBarrierRegistry` and the server's seal-strategy builders) is deleted. A cloud node seal keeps its key metadata under `<datastore.path>/node-seal/<strategy>`, needs its provider's build tag and fails closed without it; `vault` takes its token from the pre-barrier store, sealed once with the new `xkmsd seal-vault-token`. A hardware or cloud strategy that refuses the root key reports `*seal.RootKeyUnsealError`, which matches go-qrdb's `ErrInvalidCredentials`.
- **`seal.BarrierRegistry` is go-qrdb's registry, unwrapped** -- it is now a type alias for `qrdbsdk.BarrierRegistry`. `seal.WrapBarrierRegistry`, `Inner` and the forwarding methods are removed; `RegisterTenant(id)` becomes go-qrdb's `RegisterTenantDefault(id)`, and `RegisterTenantWithConfig` becomes `RegisterTenant(ctx, id, base, cfg, strategies...)`. `seal.NewBarrierRegistry` stays and refuses a nil system barrier with `ErrNilSystemBarrier`.
