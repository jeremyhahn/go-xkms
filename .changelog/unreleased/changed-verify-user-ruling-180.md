- **`local.verifyUser` names the RP and accepts the device credential (ruling 180).** `LocalVerifyUserParams` gains the required `rpName` field: 1 to 255 bytes of UTF-8, refused otherwise with `ErrVerifyUserRPName`. It is appended to the verify-user transcript after a one-byte length prefix (`BuildVerifyUserTranscript`), and the published vector's SHA-256 is now `73b6c51c106924a452dac3bd4075c2110e36f9f50b15ae3f82c3b2c0ae796c17`. The UVK policy accepts `userAuthType` 1 (device credential), 2 (biometric) or 3 (either), and refuses 0 and any other bit. Two names change with it: `RequireAndroidAttestedPerUseBiometric` becomes `RequireAndroidAttestedPerUseUserVerification`, and `ErrAndroidKeyUserAuthTypeNotBiometric` becomes `ErrAndroidKeyUserAuthTypeNotUserVerification`. `keybackend.UserVerifier.VerifyUser` now takes the token's RP ID. The authenticator passes the request's `rpId`, and `PhoneKeyBackend` sends `xkey` when the token is not RP-scoped. SPEC: `phone-verify-user`.
- **Android attestation chains are checked against Google's revocation status list, failing closed (ruling 180).** Every certificate of the presented and the verified chain, the anchor included, is looked up in the attestation certificate revocation status list; `REVOKED` and `SUSPENDED` fail with `ErrCertificateRevoked`. `AndroidDeviceTrust.Revocation` is required (`ErrNoRevocationSource`), the purpose verifier takes `WithRevocation` (SDK `WithAttestationRevocation`), the phone backend's `AttestationConfig` gains `status_list_url` and `status_list_file`, and xkey reads `phone.attestation_status_url` (default Google's list) and `phone.attestation_status_file`. A fetched list is cached for its RFC 9111 freshness; an unreadable, unreachable or stale list fails with `ErrStatusListUnavailable`, and there is no fail-open setting. SPEC: `phone-verify-user`.
