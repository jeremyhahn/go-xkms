- **`local.verifyUser` names the RP and accepts the device credential (ruling 180).** `LocalVerifyUserParams` gains the required `rpName` field: 1 to 64 bytes of UTF-8 (the WebAuthn L2 §5.4.1 RP-name bound) with no control character, refused otherwise with `ErrVerifyUserRPName` (`ValidateVerifyUserRPName`). The verify-user transcript is followed by SHA-256 of the RP name (`BuildVerifyUserTranscript`, which now takes the RP name first), binding the RP as WebAuthn L2 §6.1 binds it through `rpIdHash`; the published vector's SHA-256 moves from `30f3f98d…d11a04` to `a47b57bab3657a8ee22312c3b9d6e30517bb0470275f4140e1ef6887beaa141c` for the RP name `example.com`. The UVK policy accepts `userAuthType` 1 (device credential), 2 (biometric) or 3 (either), and refuses 0 and any other bit. Two names change with it: `RequireAndroidAttestedPerUseBiometric` becomes `RequireAndroidAttestedPerUseUserVerification`, and `ErrAndroidKeyUserAuthTypeNotBiometric` becomes `ErrAndroidKeyUserAuthTypeNotUserVerification`. `keybackend.UserVerifier.VerifyUser` now takes the token's RP ID. The authenticator passes the request's `rpId`, and `PhoneKeyBackend` sends `xkey` when the token is not RP-scoped. SPEC: `phone-verify-user`.
- **Android attestation chains are checked against Google's revocation status list, failing closed (ruling 180).** Every certificate of the presented and the verified chain, the anchor included, is looked up in the attestation certificate revocation status list; `REVOKED` and `SUSPENDED` fail with `ErrCertificateRevoked`. `AndroidDeviceTrust.Revocation` is required (`ErrNoRevocationSource`), the purpose verifier takes `WithRevocation` (SDK `WithAttestationRevocation`), the phone backend's `AttestationConfig` gains `status_list_url` and `status_list_file`, and xkey reads `phone.attestation_status_url` (default Google's list) and `phone.attestation_status_file`. A fetched list is cached for its RFC 9111 freshness; an unreadable, unreachable or stale list fails with `ErrStatusListUnavailable`, and there is no fail-open setting. SPEC: `phone-verify-user`.
