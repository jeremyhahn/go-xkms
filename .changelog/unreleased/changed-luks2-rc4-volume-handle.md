- **LUKS callers move to the go-luks2 v1.0.0-rc.4 volume API** -- `pkg/luks` and xkey's USB image code open a `*luks2.Volume` handle instead of calling the removed package-level functions, and use go-luks2's `pkg/dmcrypt`, `pkg/loop` and `pkg/filesystem` for device mapping, loop devices and filesystems. xkey's `usb.Status` and `usb.UpdateBinaries` now take a `context.Context` as their first argument.
