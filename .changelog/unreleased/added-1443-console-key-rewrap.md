- **The web console can rewrap a ciphertext.** A new Rewrap screen at `#/keys/rewrap` (Keys & Crypto, shown with `keys:decrypt`) calls `POST /api/v1/keys/rewrap` to re-encrypt a ciphertext under its key's current version or under another key and backend, without the plaintext leaving the server. It takes a bare base64 ciphertext or a pasted encrypt response, and copies the result as JSON.
