- **xkmsctl: TPM vault keys under named policies; PIV/PKCS#11 are xkey's.** xKey's local TPM policy service (registry, policy authority, NV indexes, approval counter) moved to the shared `pkg/localtpm` (`sdk/go/localtpm`), which xkey and xkmsctl both run. `xkmsctl password vault enroll --key-factor tpm --tpm-create --tpm-policy <name>` now creates the key under a named policy in `--tpm-data-dir` (which replaces `--tpm-keystore` and shares xkey's data-directory layout); `ErrVaultTPMNamedPolicy` and `ErrVaultTPMUnavailable` are gone. xkmsctl stays CGO-free with no PKCS#11 build: `--key-factor piv` and the `--key-pkcs11-*`/`--key-pin` flags are removed, and PIV/PKCS#11 factors answer `ErrVaultKeyNeedsXkey` ("use xkey for PIV/PKCS#11 factors"). `make test` now runs the pkcs11 vault tests (`test-pkcs11`) and xkey's TPM simulator tests.
