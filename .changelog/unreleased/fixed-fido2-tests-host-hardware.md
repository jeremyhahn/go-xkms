- **Two unit tests no longer depend on the FIDO2 authenticators attached to the host running them.** `TestNewHardwareAdapter_NilEnumeratorUsesPlatformDefault` (`pkg/webauthn/client`) walked the real `/sys/class/hidraw` and required that it find nothing, and `TestEnrollFIDO2Admin_ReachesDevice` (`cmd/xkmsctl`) opened the platform enumerator and required that enrollment fail at discovery. Both failed on any host with a security key plugged in: the first found a device, and the second got past discovery and failed later, at the relying-party check. The nil-enumerator fallback in `NewHardwareAdapter` is now a small function, `hidEnumerator`, whose choice of enumerator is tested by type, with no enumeration. `xkmsctl`'s `Config` gains `HIDEnumerator`, read through `Config.HIDDeviceEnumerator()` by `admin create` and the two `user` WebAuthn commands in place of their own `fido2.NewDefaultEnumerator()` calls, so the enrollment test injects an enumerator that finds no authenticator. A nil `HIDEnumerator` keeps the platform enumerator.
  - Files: `pkg/webauthn/client/hardware_adapter.go`, `cmd/xkmsctl/config.go`, `cmd/xkmsctl/admin.go`, `cmd/xkmsctl/user.go`.
  - Verified by `TestHIDEnumerator_NilUsesPlatformDefault`, `TestHIDEnumerator_KeepsTheSuppliedEnumerator`, `TestEnrollFIDO2Admin_ReachesDevice` (which now also asserts `ErrAuthenticatorNotFound`), and `TestConfig_HIDDeviceEnumerator_*`, all passing on a host with three FIDO2 devices attached.
