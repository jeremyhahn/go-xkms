- **JWK EC members at full width (#1724)** — `pkg/encoding/jwk` `FromPublicKey` / `FromPrivateKey` now encode an EC key's `x`, `y` and `d` at the curve's full size, leading zero octets included, as RFC 7518 §6.2.1.2, §6.2.1.3 and §6.2.2.1 require. Before, about one key in 128 got a short member, which strict verifiers reject and which changed its RFC 7638 thumbprint. An off-curve point or an invalid scalar now fails with `ecpoint.ErrInvalidPoint` / `ecpoint.ErrInvalidScalar`. The session signing JWK uses this encoder.
