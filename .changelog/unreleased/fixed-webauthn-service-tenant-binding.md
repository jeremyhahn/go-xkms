- **A WebAuthn user store for a non-system tenant runs in that tenant.** The store now scopes the context its `CallContext` binds to its `TenantID`, so go-qrdb's `EffectiveTenant` no longer refuses registration and email lookup with the implicit system tenant. Authority is unchanged: the bound identity must belong to the tenant. The system-tenant service principal (`node.ServiceContext`) is refused there, so a tenant store binds a tenant identity such as `node.DelegatedContext`'s.
