- **xkey appraises device evidence through the server.** With an xkmsd server configured, `xkey device attest` and `xkey tpm ek show` send the Android key attestation chain or the EK certificate they verified locally to `AppraiseAndroidKeyAttestation` / `AppraiseEKCertificate` through the sdk/go Client and print the server's verdict; a refusal exits non-zero with `ErrDeviceEvidenceRefused`. Standalone use is unchanged. sdk/go/transport now aliases `AppraiseAndroidKeyAttestationRequest` and `AppraiseEKCertificateRequest`.
