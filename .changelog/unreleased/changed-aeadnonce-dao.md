- **AEAD nonce claims are go-qrdb DAO entities, written as xkms's service principal** -- `aeadnonce.NewStore(daos)` now takes a `*qrdbsdk.DAOFactory` serving the `aeadnonce` database (`aeadnonce.DatabaseName`) instead of a `storage.Backend`, and records each claim as one `aead_nonce_claims` entity whose ID is a hash of the claimed entry. A claim is the DAO's `SaveIfAbsent`, so an entry is claimed once across every process and node sharing the datastore, never twice. xkmsd opens the database on its datastore, whose `datastore.WithCallContext` runs the claims as the embedded node's service principal. A failed claim is a `*aeadnonce.StoreError`, never a refusal; `aeadnonce.NilBackendError` is gone (`aeadnonce.ErrNilDAOFactory` replaces it). Claims written under the old `aeadnonce` storage prefix are not read.
