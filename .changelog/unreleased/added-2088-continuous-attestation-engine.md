- **Continuous attestation engine.** The new library `pkg/attestation/continuous` re-attests enrolled devices on demand (`Engine.Reattest`) and once per interval on a ticker (`Engine.Run`). Each run uses a fresh nonce and appraises the evidence with the attestation verifier. It stores the verdict per tenant (`Store.LatestVerdict`, `Store.ListVerdicts`) and publishes it to subscribers. If evidence cannot be collected or appraised, the verdict is unverified and carries a cause; the device is never skipped. No transport reaches the engine yet.
