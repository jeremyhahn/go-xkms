- **Android key attestation and EK certificate appraisal.** `AppraiseAndroidKeyAttestation` and `AppraiseEKCertificate` verify an Android key attestation chain or a TPM EK certificate under the tenant's `android-root` / `ek-root` endorsements, gated by `attestation_verifier:verify`, over embedded, REST (`POST /api/v1/attestation/appraise/android-key`, `/ek-certificate`) and MCP (`attestation.appraise.android_key`, `attestation.appraise.ek_certificate`); USB answers not supported.
