- **xkmsd mTLS takes the tenant by qrdb's certificate-to-tenant rule, not Subject OU[0] (breaking).** A client certificate that carries a Subject OU names a tenant only through `qrdbsdk.TenantFromClientCert` under the new `auth.MTLSConfig.TrustDomain` (the server passes its `TrustDomain()`): the first OU and the SPIFFE SAN trust domain must agree, so an OU-only certificate, an OU naming another tenant than the SAN, or a tenant that is the cluster trust domain is refused with `qrdbsdk.ErrInvalidClientCertificate`. A bound user of another tenant than the certificate names is refused with `auth.CertificateTenantMismatchError` (the ACME admin type now aliases it). A certificate with no OU claims no tenant, as before (#2086).
