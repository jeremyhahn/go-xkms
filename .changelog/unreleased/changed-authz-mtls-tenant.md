- **xkmsd mTLS takes the tenant by qrdb's certificate-to-tenant rule, not Subject OU[0] (breaking).** A client certificate that carries a Subject OU names a tenant only through `qrdbsdk.TenantFromClientCert` under the new `auth.MTLSConfig.TrustDomain` (the server passes its `TrustDomain()`): the first OU and the SPIFFE SAN trust domain must agree, so an OU-only certificate, an OU naming another tenant than the SAN, or a tenant that is the cluster trust domain is refused with `qrdbsdk.ErrInvalidClientCertificate`. Bound-user mTLS authentication (a `UserStore`) is unchanged. On the certificate-only path (no `UserStore`) a certificate with no Subject OU is now refused too, where it used to authenticate as a tenantless identity: before upgrading, replace every such client credential with one carrying the tenant OU and its SPIFFE SAN (#2086).
