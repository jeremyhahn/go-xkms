- **go-qrdb and its dependency set move to go-qrdb v0.47.0-alpha** -- the root, `sdk/go`, `xkey` and `web/desktop` modules now require `go-qrdb` and `go-qrdb/sdk/go` v0.47.0-alpha, `go-enclave` v0.8.0-alpha, `go-luks2` v1.0.0-rc.4, `go-truststrap` v0.6.0-alpha and `go-objstore` v0.4.0-alpha, and Go selects `go-quicraft` v0.20.0 through go-qrdb. Callers follow the new APIs: `secure.Buffer` is read through `WithBytes`, `secure.NewSecret` and `NewLiteralSecretValue` take a context, pairing static keys are read through `PublicBytes` and `pairing.WithPrivateKey`, and the error from wiping seal credentials (`Credentials.Secret.Destroy`) is now returned or logged instead of dropped. The PIN-TPM2 backend's FIDO2 PIN-hash check handles the error go-enclave's `EqualTo` now returns, and fails closed on it.
