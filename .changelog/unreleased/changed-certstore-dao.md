- **The certificate store stores go-qrdb DAO entities** -- `certstore.New` now takes the go-qrdb DAO factory (`certstore.Config.DAOs`) instead of a `storage.Backend`. Certificates and chains are `certificates` and `certificate_chains` entities in the CA's own `ca` database (`certstore.DatabaseName`, re-exported as `xkms.CertStoreDatabase`), keyed by `certstore.CertificateKey`. A miss is a `*certstore.NotFoundError` that matches `ErrCertNotFound` / `ErrChainNotFound` and wraps the DAO's typed not-found error, which `pkg/ca` maps to `ErrCertificateNotFound`; any other failure is a `*certstore.StoreError`. `compose.CAParams` gains a required `DAOs` field, and `compose.Databases(deps)` names the `ca` database when a CA is configured. A factory that does not serve it fails with `*dao.DatabaseUnavailableError`. `xkmsd` opens the database on its shared datastore, and the `xkmsd init` bootstrap CA stores its certificate through an in-memory factory. The file/KV certificate store is gone, and existing certificate data is not migrated.
