- **A PIV invite activation whose commit outcome is unknown keeps the first-credential claim.** An `ActivateWithCertBinding` error other than "account not pending" no longer releases the claim: the account is read back, and the claim is released only while it is pending with no certificate binding, committed when the commit activated it, and kept when the read fails, so a running WebAuthn invite finish cannot enrol a passkey on a certificate-bound account.
- **An interrupted recovery of an incomplete TPM key pair leaves no mismatched pair.** `SaveKeyPair` fences the public blob with a value `LoadKeyPair` refuses before it rewrites the private blob, and writes the public blob last; a create-only save that meets a fence refuses, and deleting the key or saving it with overwrite repairs it. A recovery whose rewrite fails keeps its fence rather than deleting the key, so a live writer paused between its writes cannot complete a public blob with no private blob beside it.
- **The invitation ceremony's fenced first-credential write goes through the configured `CredentialStore.Save`.** A decorator that embeds the store and overrides `Save` now sees invitation registrations; the claim rides in the context to the built-in store.
- **An invitation registration interrupted between saving its passkey and committing its claim resumes with that passkey.** Once the claim's lease has expired, a credential read rolls the claim forward (under the same token compare-and-swap) when a credential was saved under its token, so the retry offers a sign-in ceremony for the saved key instead of asking the authenticator to create another.
