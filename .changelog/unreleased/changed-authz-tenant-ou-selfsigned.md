- **PIV self-signed and xkmsd bootstrap certificates name their tenant as `Subject.OrganizationalUnit[0]`.** A PIV slot self-signed certificate (`GeneratePIVKey`, `SelfSignExistingPIVKey`) carries the caller's tenant, or the system tenant without an identity, as its only OU; the xkmsd bootstrap listener certificate names the system tenant, and a persisted one without it is regenerated at the next start. `StampTenant` and its errors now live in the leaf package `pkg/certsubject`; `ca.StampTenant`, `ca.ErrTenantRequired` and `ca.ErrInvalidRawSubject` are aliases of them (SPEC `authz-tenant-ou`, #2089).
