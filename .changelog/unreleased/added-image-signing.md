- **Container images are signed and verified with KMS keys.** `pkg/image` signs an image with a KMS `crypto.Signer` certified under `TrustedCA`'s code-signing profile, producing a DSSE envelope over an in-toto statement that names the image digest. The envelope is pushed both as an OCI 1.1 referrer and into cosign's `sha256-<hex>.att` tag, with an optional RFC 3161 timestamp. `VerifyImage` checks an image's attestations against the trusted image-signer roots, at the timestamp's time when one verifies. The registry client follows a registry's Bearer token challenge and every page of the referrers index, and a timestamp is trusted only from a TSA certificate whose sole extended key usage is a critical id-kp-timeStamping. `pkg/signing` gains `Verify`, the RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 check over SHA-256, SHA-384 or SHA-512 through `types.SimpleVerifier`. The SDK exposes both as `xkms.ImageVerifier` and `xkms.VerifySignature`. SPEC: image-signing.
