- **ACME finalize issues through `pkg/acme/issuance`: goodkey, a reserved serial, and linted precertificate and certificate** — a weak CSR key is refused as `badPublicKey`; the CA lints each tbsCertificate with zlint before signing it (CA/Browser Forum Baseline Requirements §4.3.1.2), refusing an error or fatal finding as `badCSR` and logging and auditing warnings (`CERTIFICATE_LINTED`); the precertificate and the certificate share one reserved serial and must match apart from the RFC 6962 §3.1 poison and SCT list extensions. xkmsd runs `linter.PrivatePKIConfig` plus the operator's `acme.lint.exclude_lints` / `exclude_sources`. The CA's default key usage asserts `keyEncipherment` for RSA subject keys only (RFC 5480 §3, RFC 8410 §5).
