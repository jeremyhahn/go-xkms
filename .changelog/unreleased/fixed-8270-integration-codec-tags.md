- **The sshagent and fido2 integration suites build with the codec tags.** `make integration-test-sshagent` and `make integration-test-fido2` now pass `codec_cbor codec_json codec_msgpack` to their builds, as `integration-test-sdk-go` does. Without them the stores the suites open failed with `codec "json" is not supported in this build`, which the `Integration` job would have hit at the next target once the module fetch was fixed. A build-gate test keeps the two recipes on the codec tags.
