- **A Local Attestation Key (LAK), certified by the IAK, carries the WebAuthn Level 2 §8.3.1 TPM attestation profile; the IAK's TCG DevID certificate is unchanged.** TCG DevID v1 r12/v1.10 r9 §8 requires the IAK certificate's Subject to be a unique X.500 DN; WebAuthn §8.3.1 requires the "tpm" attestation statement's AIK certificate Subject to be empty, and go-webauthn enforces this literally. Resolved per TCG "TPM 2.0 Keys for Device Identity and Attestation" v1.10 r9 §5.3/§6.3 ("Owner Creation of a LAK Certificate based on an IAK Certificate"): `pkg/tpm2.(*TPM2).CreateLAK` creates the LAK as a Restricted, Sign-only child of the Platform SRK (not an Endorsement-hierarchy primary like the IAK/IDevID) and persists it via `TPM2_EvictControl`; `CertifyLAK` has the IAK certify it via `TPM2_Certify`; `ca.IssueLAKCertificate` verifies that evidence (the IAK certificate chains to a trusted signer and is within its validity period, then `tpm2.VerifyCertifyByCertificate`) and issues a certificate with an empty Subject, a critical subjectAltName directoryName carrying tcg-at-tpmManufacturer/tpmModel/tpmVersion, the tcg-kp-AIKCertificate (2.23.133.8.3) EKU alone, and Basic Constraints CA:false (`ca.NewTCGLocalAttestationKeyProfile`). xkey's TPM-backed WebAuthn/FIDO2 authenticator (`keybackend.TPM2AttestingBackend`) now attests with the LAK via `CertifyKeyWithLAK`, never the IAK. `pkg/certstore`'s `StoreCertificate`/`StoreCertificateChain` key a certificate with an empty Subject by its SPKI SHA-256 thumbprint (`certstore.CertificateKey`, using qrdb's canonical `ComputeSPKIThumbprint`) instead of refusing it with `ErrInvalidCN`. Verified on the TPM simulator: LAK creation and IAK-TPM2_Certify-LAK, `ca.IssueLAKCertificate`'s success and failure paths (tampered certify info, wrong IAK, an IAK not chained to a trusted root, missing device attributes, a non-empty-Subject certificate refused the way go-webauthn's own verifier refuses it), and an end-to-end "tpm" attestation statement produced from the LAK verifying under go-webauthn. See `docs/tpm2/engineering.md` and `docs/certificate-authority/engineering.md`. (go-xkms#715)
