- **xkms no longer re-hosts go-qrdb's auth-factor and cluster services** -- the REST `/api/v1/factors/*` routes, `GET /api/v1/cluster/status` and `GET /api/v1/cluster/members`, their `XKMSService` passthroughs, the TypeScript SDK's `FactorsClient` and `ClusterClient`, and the web console's Auth Factors and Cluster pages are removed. go-qrdb's `UserAuthFactorService` and `AdminService` serve them, registered on xkmsd's gRPC server and Unix socket; use the qrdb SDK client's `AuthFactors()` / `UserAuthFactors()` and `Cluster()` accessors, or `xkmsctl auth factor` and `xkmsctl cluster`. `xkms.ClusterClient` shrinks to the leader-endpoint lookup the not-leader redirect needs, and `XKMSService.SetClusterClient` drops its node-ID argument. The `/auth/me` `cluster` capability flag stays, and the console dashboard renders it as "clustered" or "standalone"; the `factors` capability flag is gone.
- **The `manual` and `barrier` credential strategies are go-qrdb's credential service** -- `pkg/server/credentials` now delegates them to `qrdbsdk.NewVersionedCredentialService` instead of carrying its own in-memory and barrier stores, and keeps only the hardware platform strategies (`tpm2`, `pkcs11`, `awskms`, `gcpkms`, `azurekv`, `vault`, `passphrase`). Behaviour follows go-qrdb's contract: a manual credential is held in a sealed enclave, a barrier read failure reports `ErrCredentialNotFound` and deleting a barrier credential also removes its version history. Stored barrier credentials keep the same `credentials/` keys and stay readable. `credentials.ErrManualEntryRequired`, which nothing returned, is removed.
- **The init ceremony's go-qrdb state aliases are removed** -- `pkg/init`'s `CeremonyState`, `StateAwaitingInit`, `StateEnrolling` and `StateOperational`, and the SDK's `InitCeremonyState` and `InitState*` re-exports, are gone. Use go-qrdb's `qrdbsdk.CeremonyState` and its `State*` constants directly. `pkg/init` keeps `SPKIPin`, `CAAdapter`, `EnrollAdminFactors` and `NewAdminFactorRegistry`.
