- **CA blobs live in a go-qrdb storage engine; go-xkms no longer requires go-objstore directly (#1556).** `ca.Params.ObjectStore` and `ObjectStoreFactory` now take a `qrdbsdk.Storage`, and the backend is chosen by configuration through go-qrdb. That covers cross-signed certificates, distribution-point CRLs and attestation evidence. A missing blob is `ca.ErrObjectNotFound`, and an engine failure wraps `ca.ErrStorageError`. In `sdk/go`, `ObjectStorage` is `qrdbsdk.Storage`, opened by `NewObjectStorage(provider, settings)` or `NewObjectStorageMemory()`. The go-objstore aliases (`ObjectMetadata`, `ObjectListOptions`, `ObjectInfo`, `ObjectListResult`, `ObjectArchiver`, `ObjectLifecyclePolicy`) and `NewObjectStorageLocal` are deleted with no replacement; open the local provider with `NewObjectStorage("local", map[string]string{"path": dir})`. go-objstore is now only an indirect requirement of the root and `sdk/go` modules, and the architecture gate rejects any direct import.
