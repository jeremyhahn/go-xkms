- **Key policy and crypto utilities in `xkms.v1`.** go-quicbaas served key policy and crypto utilities over its own `quicbaas.kms.v1` services, which wrapped the go-xkms library; with those wrappers unregistered its clients failed with `unknown service`. `xkms.v1` KeystoreService now serves the crypto utilities (Rewrap, EncryptBatch, DecryptBatch, GenerateDataKey, GenerateWrappedDataKey, GenerateHMAC, VerifyHMAC, GenerateRandom, Hash) on gRPC, unix, REST, QUIC, MCP and the embedded transport, each with its permission and tenant-scoped, reachable through `sdk/go` for go-quicbaas to re-export; a hash or HMAC the standard library refuses under `GODEBUG=fips140=only` fails with a typed InvalidArgument error. It also serves the key-management RPCs the same way: GetPublicKey, RevokeKey, UpdateKeyStatus, Get/UpdateKeyMetadata, Set/Get/DeleteKeyPolicy, Set/Get/DeleteKeyRotationPolicy, Set/ClearKeySafety, SafetyCheck, SafetyReport, GetSupportedAlgorithms, BackendStatus and BackendTest. Key policies, rotation policies, AEAD usage limits (NIST SP 800-38D §8.3, RFC 8439 §4), status and metadata persist in the go-qrdb datastore through the `pkg/keypolicy` DAO store, and a service without that store refuses them with a typed Unavailable error. Every key-use path is admitted through the key's status and policy: sign, verify, encrypt, decrypt, MAC, seal, unseal, derive, ECDH, wrap and unwrap by ID, and export, export of key material, copy and the key a wrap releases refuse a revoked or disabled key and an operation its policy does not allow, and wrapping under an AES-GCM key counts toward its usage limits; a key whose policy restricts its operations is never exported. AEAD usage counters belong to one key version, so a rotation racing an encryption never erases a use charged to the new version; deleting a key deletes its policy record, so a key created again under the same ID starts enabled with no inherited metadata, limits or counters; BackendTest never creates the default sealing key (a backend without one is refused with a typed error) and admits its seal and unseal through the key's status, policy and usage count. GetPublicKey returns the public key of a cloud KMS key whose private key never leaves the KMS, and of an X25519 key. GetSupportedAlgorithms and the Algorithms RPC both list the key algorithms each backend declares in its capabilities (`types.KeyAlgorithms`: RSA sizes, ECDSA curves, Ed25519, X25519, AEADs, HMAC hashes, ML-DSA and ML-KEM parameter sets) instead of expanding generic capability flags, so they agree, Azure Key Vault no longer advertises Ed25519 and AWS KMS lists AES-256-GCM only; under `GODEBUG=fips140=only` GetSupportedAlgorithms leaves out ChaCha20-Poly1305, XChaCha20-Poly1305 and X25519. DEK management is go-qrdb's `DEKClient` (#1681).
