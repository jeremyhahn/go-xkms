oidc/credential: tests now cover what happens when the refresh lease or the scheduler fails. A refused lease acquire blocks refresh, device login and expiry recording, and leaves the credential unchanged. A failed lease release keeps the refreshed tokens. Device-authorisation refusals and a corrupt DPoP key fail cleanly. The refresh sweep skips a sealed tenant. The scheduler reports a refused campaign, a failed refresh, a lost lease, and a lease it could neither renew nor release, and then carries on (#1794).
