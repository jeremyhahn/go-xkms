- **REST login rate limiting keys on the real client IP, and every REST body is bounded** (#1173). The limiter used by `/api/v1/auth/*` and `/api/v1/webauthn/*` believed the first `X-Forwarded-For` / `X-Real-IP` value from any caller and otherwise keyed on `RemoteAddr` *with its port*, so rotating the header or opening a new connection gave a fresh bucket, and every value was kept for 30 minutes. Bodies had no bound at all, including on unauthenticated routes.
  - `pkg/ratelimit` now owns the one client-IP resolver, `TrustedProxies.ClientIP` (`clientip.go`): the peer IP without its port, or, only when the peer is a configured trusted proxy, the right-most untrusted `X-Forwarded-For` hop (then `X-Real-IP`). Header values that are not IP addresses are ignored. `ratelimit.Middleware` takes the resolver; the ACME server's `ClientIP` now delegates to it, replacing its own exact-match copy (its `TrustedProxies` is now a `*ratelimit.TrustedProxies`, and a multi-hop chain now resolves to the right-most untrusted hop instead of the client-controlled left-most one).
  - New config key `server.trusted_proxies` (IPs or CIDR prefixes; empty, the default, trusts none), validated at load.
  - The limiter tracks at most `Config.MaxClients` clients (default 100,000); while full it refuses new clients (`*ratelimit.CapacityError` from `Wait`) until idle ones are cleaned up.
  - `rest.BodyLimitMiddleware` bounds every route on both mounts (legacy router and the unified server's `BindRoute`): 1 MiB by default, 4 MiB for sign, verify, encrypt, decrypt, seal and unseal (`RouteSpec.MaxBodyBytes`). An oversized body gets 413 with `RequestTooLargeError`, whether its length was declared or streamed.
  - The REST `http.Server` sets `ReadHeaderTimeout` (10 s default, `rest.Config.ReadHeaderTimeout`).
  - Tests: `pkg/ratelimit/clientip_test.go`, `pkg/api/rest/middleware_bodylimit_test.go`, `pkg/config/trusted_proxies_test.go`, `pkg/server/rest_limits_test.go`, `test/integration/ratelimit`.
