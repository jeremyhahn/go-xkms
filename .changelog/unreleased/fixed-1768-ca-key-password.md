CA: `NewCA`, `NewParentCA` and `NewIntermediateCA` carry the configured CA key password (`KeyConfig.Password`, or the identity's `Keystores.Software.Password`) into the identity the CA rebuilds from its flat config, so a CA with `RequireKeyPassword` initializes instead of failing with `ErrKeyPasswordRequired`, and its software keys are written and reloaded as encrypted PKCS#8 (#1768).
