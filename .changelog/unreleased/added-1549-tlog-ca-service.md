- **CA transparency log over go-qrdb's verifiable log** — `pkg/ca/tlog` appends certificate-issued, image-signed and revoked entries in a canonical version-1 encoding. It serves entries, inclusion proofs and consistency proofs, and returns signed tree heads verified under the CA key. `HeadSignerProvider` is go-qrdb's head-signer seam over the CA key (Ed25519 or P-256). The embedded node's service principal is granted `vlog:create`, `vlog:append`, `vlog:get`, `vlog:head`, `vlog:sign_head` and `vlog:proof`. Documented in `docs/transparency-log/engineering.md`.
- **Issuance and revocation append to the CA's transparency log** — `(*CA).SetTransparencyLog` wires a log into the CA. Every issued certificate is appended once before it is stored or returned, and a failed append fails the issuance (`ErrTransparencyLogAppend`). Every revocation is appended after it is recorded; a failed append leaves the revocation in place and returns `ErrTransparencyLogEntryMissing`. `qrdb_vlog` joins `TEST_TAGS` and `BUILD_TAGS`, so CI runs the node-backed tests.
- **Image signatures append to the transparency log** — when `image.SignerConfig.TransparencyLog` is set, every signature is appended as an `image-signed` entry before it is pushed. A failed append pushes nothing (`image.ErrTransparencyLogAppend`). `tlog.OpenOrCreate` opens a log, or creates it on first use.
- **xkmsd attaches the CA's transparency log** — the new `transparency_log.enabled` setting is on by default in builds with `qrdb_vlog` and off without it. `qrdb_vlog` joins `SERVER_BUILD_TAGS`, so an xkmsd built by `make` has the log on. `transparency_log.origin` defaults to `<CA common name>/tlog`. Either way, xkmsd builds and runs. The embedded node gets the CA key's head signer, and `initializeCA` opens the `ca` log before the TLS server certificate is issued. Every append runs as the node's service principal. If the switch is on but the log cannot be opened, start-up fails with `server.ErrTransparencyLog`.
