- **CA transparency log over go-qrdb's verifiable log** — `pkg/ca/tlog` appends certificate-issued, image-signed and revoked entries in a canonical version-1 encoding, serves entries and inclusion and consistency proofs, and returns signed tree heads verified under the CA key. `HeadSignerProvider` is go-qrdb's head-signer seam over the CA key (Ed25519 or P-256). The embedded node's service principal is granted `vlog:create`, `vlog:append`, `vlog:get`, `vlog:head`, `vlog:sign_head` and `vlog:proof`. Documented in `docs/transparency-log/engineering.md`.
