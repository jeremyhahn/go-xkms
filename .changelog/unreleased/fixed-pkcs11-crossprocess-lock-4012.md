- **A file-store writer no longer starves waiting for the cross-process lock (DS-4012)** — `withCrossProcessLock` polled a non-blocking `flock`/`LockFileEx` on a 1 ms timer, so while other writers kept the store busy (each holding the lock across the engine's sync) a writer that released and re-acquired at once won every time and a polling waiter could miss every release until its context deadline: `TestModuleStorage_FailedAttemptCounter_CrossProcessAccumulates` failed in CI with `acquiring cross-process lock: context deadline exceeded` after 10 s. A contended lock is now waited for in the kernel, which wakes the waiter the moment the holder releases; the wait runs on its own goroutine so the context still bounds it, and a wait abandoned at the deadline releases the lock as soon as it is granted (`pkg/storage/file/lock.go`, `lock_unix.go`, `lock_windows.go`, tests in `crosslock_test.go`).
