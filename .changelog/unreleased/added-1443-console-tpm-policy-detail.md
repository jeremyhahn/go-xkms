- **Web console TPM Policy Detail view.** `#/tpm/policies/detail` (Keys & Crypto, shown where the deployment has the `tpm_policies` capability) picks the caller's own scope or a tenant, loads one TPM policy with each branch's conditions, captured PCR values and digest, lists the keys and sealed objects bound to it and verifies it against the live PCRs, naming each mismatched PCR. The view is read-only.
