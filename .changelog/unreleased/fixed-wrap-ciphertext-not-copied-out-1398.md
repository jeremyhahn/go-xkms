- **AES-KW, AES-KWP and RSA-AES wrap emit ciphertext as ordinary bytes** -- the last of the six RFC 3394 passes now writes its result into a plain slice instead of the wrapped key being copied out of the protected register buffer with `secure.CopyOut`, so callers of `WrapAESKW`, `WrapAESKWP` and `WrapRSAAES` no longer inherit a copy-out obligation on public data and `WrapRSAAES` no longer wipes the ciphertext it assembles. Output bytes are unchanged (RFC 3394 and RFC 5649 vectors pass). Part of #1398.
