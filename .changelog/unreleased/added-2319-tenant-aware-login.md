- **Tenant-aware passkey login (#2319)**: a passkey login begins on the relying party of the tenant named by `tenant_id` or `X-Tenant-Id`, or by the request host's `tenant_hosts` entry. The account lookup is scoped to that tenant, and the minted token carries it. An unknown or cross-tenant request gets an indistinguishable decoy whose finish fails like a wrong assertion. Tenant invitations enrol through their tenant's relying party.
