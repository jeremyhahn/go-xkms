- **A slow TPM no longer reads as a missing one for the node seal** -- the `tpm2` and `pkcs11` node seals now open the configured TPM through the new `seal.OpenTPM2Strategy`, bounded at 30 seconds instead of the autodetect's 3, and keep the reason in the chain as a `*seal.TPM2ProbeError`. That reason is the backend's open error, `seal.ErrTPM2ProbeTimeout` or `seal.ErrTPM2NotSealCapable`. Before this, a TPM that took longer than 3 seconds to open (a loaded host, the simulator under the race detector) failed startup with "no usable, provisioned TPM was found". `seal.BuildBestStrategy` keeps its fall-back behaviour. Files: `pkg/seal/autodetect.go`, `errors.go`, `pkg/embeddednode/embedded_node.go`.
