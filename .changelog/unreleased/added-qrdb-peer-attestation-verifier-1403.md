- **`pkg/attestation/peergate`: the verifier behind go-qrdb's attested-caller gate, with the evidence cache it reads and the collector that fills it.** go-qrdb's `PeerAttestationGate` (installed with `node.WithPeerAttestationGate`) asks a `PeerAttestationVerifier` whether the peer on a gated connection is currently attested; go-xkms now supplies it, so a host composes the gate from the two SDKs and writes no verification code. `Collector.Challenge` issues a single-use 32-byte nonce to the peer named by the connection's verified client certificate (go-qrdb's SPKI thumbprint), `Collector.Submit` accepts the evidence answering it, bound to the submitting TLS session through the exporter and stamped with the receipt time, into an `EvidenceStore` (`MemoryStore`: bounded, evicting only evidence past its retention, never aliasing). `Verifier.VerifyPeer` re-verifies the stored evidence on every call with `pkg/attestation/peer` under the provisioned policy bound to the session it arrived on, and maps the verdict onto go-qrdb's absence/cryptographic classes; with `DeviceIdentity` set it also requires the connection certificate to be an IDevID naming the same TPM as the attestation key (new `peer.VerifySameDevice`, `*peer.DeviceMismatchError`), closing the relay a channel binding alone leaves open. `QuoteEvidence` is the quoting side over any `Quoter` (`*tpm2.TPM2` is one) and re-quotes when a PCR is extended between the quote and the read. Exported through `sdk/go` (`NewPeerGateVerifier`, `NewPeerEvidenceCollector`, `NewPeerMemoryEvidenceStore`, `PeerQuoteEvidence`, `PeerVerifySameDevice` and their types and errors). `make integration-test-peergate` composes a go-qrdb node with the verifier and drives quotes from swtpm: the gated route admits the peer after a valid bound quote and refuses it with no evidence, after a forged quote, after a quote bound to another TLS session and with evidence older than the gate's maximum age. Docs: `docs/attested-peer/`. Exported-API growth.
