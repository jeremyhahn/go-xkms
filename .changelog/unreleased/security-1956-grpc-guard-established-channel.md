- **The gRPC secure-channel guard judges the channel `Connect` established, not the caller's mutable `Config`.** Setting `TLSEnabled` on the `Config` after a plaintext `Connect` no longer lets `SignImage` send registry credentials, or a leader redirect redial, over the plaintext channel: once connected, the guard refuses unless `AllowInsecureTransport` is still set, which it reads at call time (go-xkms#1956).
