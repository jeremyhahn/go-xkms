- **No secret or authorization value is logged, at any level** -- `pkg/tpm2` no longer logs the SO PIN / hierarchy authorization, hierarchy passwords, sealed HMAC secrets, NV secrets or credential-activation secrets (raw or hex) at Debug, and the `tpm2.Params.DebugSecrets` option that enabled that logging is removed. Lengths of auth values are secret-derived too: `CreateSRK` logs `hierarchy_auth_set`/`user_auth_set` presence flags instead of `hierarchy_auth_len`/`user_auth_len`, and the platform-password unseal byte count, the CA enrollment `secret_size`, the CTAP2 `pinUvAuthParam_len`/`pinHashEnc_len` and xkey pairing's `secretLen` are no longer logged (CA m417).
