- **Hybrid leaf certificates name their tenant as `Subject.OrganizationalUnit[0]`.** `ca.CreateHybridCertificate` takes a leading `tenantID`: a leaf template is stamped with it, replacing any OU the template names, and an empty tenant on a leaf is refused with `ErrTenantRequired`; a CA template keeps its subject. The virtual-authenticator attestation CSR is exempt, its Subject-OU fixed by WebAuthn Level 2 §8.2.1 (SPEC `authz-tenant-ou`, #2089).
- **Tenant CA templates carry the tenant OU; `ca.CheckOwnerOU` refuses a tenant subject without it (`ErrOwnerOUMissing`).** FIDO2 attestation, TCG LAK and system root/intermediate subjects are exempt by profile or template kind (ruling 1073 (a), #2089).
