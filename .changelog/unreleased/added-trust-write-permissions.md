- **Trust write permissions `trust:publish_dane` and `trust:auto_import_aia`.** go-xkms assigned no permission to DANE TLSA publication or AIA issuer import, so a consumer serving those verbs (go-quicbaas's `POST /api/v1/trust/publish-dane` and `/api/v1/trust/auto-import-aia`) had to refuse them on every transport. `pkg/auth` now declares `ResourceTrust` with `ActionPublishDANE` and `ActionAutoImportAIA`; the service module registers both, cross-scoped and of governance duty, and grants them to Admin (and so Owner), like the `trust_roots` writes. sdk/go re-exports the names as `PermissionResourceTrust`, `PermissionActionPublishDANE` and `PermissionActionAutoImportAIA`. go-xkms's own REST, gRPC and MCP surfaces serve neither verb.
