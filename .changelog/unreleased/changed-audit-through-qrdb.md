- **go-xkms audits through go-qrdb's audit logger** -- `pkg/audit` no longer ships its own sink: the chained-file, qrdb and no-op loggers, the `Logger` interface and the severity classification (`HandleWriteFailure`, `ClassifyAction`) are deleted. Every product event goes through `audit.Emit` into go-qrdb's `NodeAuditLogger` (the node's own trail, or `NewAuditFileLogger` when no node is embedded), and a failed write surfaces as go-qrdb's `*AuditGateError` so the request fails closed. A nil sink now means auditing is off. Events stored by the old chained-file logger are not read by the new one.
