- **xkey's embedded PKCS#11 transport can now wrap and unwrap keys by ID** (#1195). `EmbeddedTransport.WrapKeyByID`/`UnwrapKeyByID` (the `C_WrapKey`/`C_UnwrapKey` primitives) routed through the key-IMPORT ceremony instead -- `xkms.ExportKey` with an empty algorithm, an unexported `getImportParams` helper, and `xkms.WrapKey`/`UnwrapKey` -- which always wraps under a freshly generated ephemeral key pair, never the caller's own designated wrapping key, and never threaded the resulting `ImportToken` back in. The ceremony could not succeed for this use and would have double-wrapped material even once the token plumbing was fixed.
  - `pkg/xkms.WrapKeyByID`/`UnwrapKeyByID` (`pkg/xkms/service.go`) are new process-singleton free functions, alongside `ExportKey`/`WrapKey`/`UnwrapKey`/`GetImportParameters`, that delegate to the already-correct `XKMSService.WrapKeyByID`/`UnwrapKeyByID` (`pkg/xkms/servicer_key.go`) -- the same implementation the daemon transport (`pkg/api/transport/embedded/transport.go`) already used.
  - Re-exported from `sdk/go` as `xkms.WrapKeyByID`/`UnwrapKeyByID` (`sdk/go/xkms_service.go`).
  - `xkey/pkg/pkcs11.EmbeddedTransport.WrapKeyByID`/`UnwrapKeyByID` now delegate to those SDK re-exports directly, encrypting the target key material under the caller's own designated wrapping key (RFC 3394 AES Key Wrap, RSA-OAEP, and every other mechanism `pkg/xkms/servicer_wrapkey.go` supports) instead of an ephemeral one. The now-dead `getImportParams` helper is removed.
  - Files: `pkg/xkms/service.go`, `sdk/go/xkms_service.go`, `xkey/pkg/pkcs11/service.go`, `xkey/docs/pkcs11/engineering.md`.
  - Verified by `pkg/xkms/service_wrapkeybyid_test.go` (free-function delegation, `ErrNotInitialized` before `Initialize`) and `xkey/pkg/pkcs11/service_embeddedtransport_test.go` (AES-KW and RSA-OAEP wrap/unwrap round trips proving the unwrapped material matches the original; nil-request, unresolvable-key and unsupported-algorithm error paths).
