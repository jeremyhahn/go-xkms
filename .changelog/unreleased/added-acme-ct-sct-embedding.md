- **ACME CT precertificates and SCT embedding at finalize** — with a CT log enabled under `acme.ct` (xkmsd and the TrustedCA), finalize submits each precertificate to every enabled log (RFC 6962 §4.2 add-pre-chain), waits for all logs, verifies each SCT, enforces `min_scts`, `min_operators` and `require_rfc6962_log`, and embeds the verified SCTs in the final certificate as the RFC 6962 §3.3 SCT list extension; each submission is audited as `CERTIFICATE_TRANSPARENCY`, an unmet policy fails issuance with a `serverInternal` problem (RFC 8555 §6.7) whose detail names the unmet requirement and whether a log refused or timed out (never a log's URL), and submissions run behind the `acme.resilience.ct_submitter` circuit breaker. EK certificates (endorse-01) and TCG-CSR-IDEVID IAK/IDevID certificates are never CT-logged (RFC 6962 §1; TCG DevID v1.1 §3.11, §11); device-attest-01 certificates are logged when CT is enabled.
