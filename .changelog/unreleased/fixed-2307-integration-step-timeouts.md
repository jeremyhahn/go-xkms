- **Every Integration job step has its own time bound, and the pool registry address stays masked in every job.** A hung image build or suite step in the Integration job now fails at its step bound (30 minutes for the image steps, 45 for `make integration-test`) instead of at the 180-minute job bound (#2307). Each job that pulls the pool toolchain image masks the registry, its host:port and its bare host before the pull, because masks set in `ci-images` do not carry into other jobs. The `scripts/ci-registry.sh` shell tests no longer pick up `CI_POOL=true` from the lint-gates job's environment.
