- **`enroll-01` finalize did not bind the TCG-CSR-IDEVID to the TPM that passed credential activation** (#1391, #1370 review). Activation at the challenge proves a TPM holding one endorsement key and one activation key answered, and nothing recorded which. The TCG-CSR arrives one request later, so a client that passed the challenge once could submit one built in software around another device's *public* EK certificate and be issued an IDevID carrying that device's identity.
  - **The challenge records the activated TPM.** `enroll01.VerifyActivation` (new; `Verify` is now a wrapper) returns the `Activation` (the EK certificate and the IAK's TPMT_PUBLIC read from the CSR the credential was made for), and the enroll-01 attesting verifier returns it as `AttestedIdentity.Attestation`, persisted on the challenge as an `entities.DeviceAttestation` with `Format: "enroll-01"`: `DeviceIdentityValue` is the EK certificate's TCG permanent identifier, `LeafDER` the certificate, `PubArea` the IAK. The identifier comparison is kept and is real for enroll-01: the dial that proved a genuine TPM answered reached the address the identifier names.
  - **Finalize refuses a TCG-CSR for any other TPM.** `bindEnrollActivation` requires the CSR's EK certificate to have the recorded permanent identifier and its IAK public area to be the recorded one; no record is refused too (`ErrEnrollActivationNotFound`, `ErrEnrollActivationMismatch`, `badCSR`). New `ca.EKCertificatePermanentIdentifier` is the single derivation of the identifier, shared with the IAK/IDevID issuance (`sdk/go` `EKCertificatePermanentIdentifier`).
  - **The validated challenge decides the finalize request** (`validatedAttestations.requireOnly`, `ErrFinalizeRequestNotPermitted`): a TCG-CSR-IDEVID needs enroll-01 to be the only attestation challenge that validated the order. An `endorse-01` or `device-attest-01` authorization can no longer be finalized as a TCG-CSR, and neither can an order no attestation challenge validated (an `ip` identifier `http-01` validated, which was issued an IDevID with no TPM check). **Behavior change** for any client that finalized a TCG-CSR for such an order.
  - SDK: `ACMEVerifyEnroll01Activation`, `ACMEEnroll01Activation`, `ACMEEnroll01Format`, `ACMEErrEnroll01ActivationUnreadable`, `ACMEErrEnrollActivationNotFound`, `ACMEErrEnrollActivationMismatch`, `ACMEErrFinalizeRequestNotPermitted`.
  - Verified by `TestOrderFinalize_TCGCSR_AnotherTPMsEKIsRefused` and `TestOrderFinalize_TCGCSR_AnotherIAKIsRefused` (activate TPM A, finalize a TCG-CSR built around TPM B's EK certificate or a software IAK; both fail without the fix), `TestVerifyActivation_*`, `TestBindEnrollActivation` and `TestACMEAttestingVerifiers_Enroll01RecordsTheActivatedTPM`. Docs: `docs/acme/challenges.md`.
