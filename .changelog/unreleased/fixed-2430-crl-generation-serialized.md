- **Back-to-back CRL generations no longer collide.** The CA now serializes CRL generation, so reading the previous CRL number, signing, auditing and writing the CRL artifact happen as one step. Before, overlapping generations (`ca crl`, the refresh after a revocation, and the regenerate-on-read of a stale CRL) could read the same previous number. They then issued duplicate CRL numbers and raced on the artifact write, and the losing write answered `ca crl` with a bare internal server error.
