- **Continuous attestation verdicts on REST.** The xkms service now serves the verdicts of the continuous attestation engine. `GET /api/v1/attestation/verdicts?device=` lists a device's verdicts (`attestation_verdicts:list`), `GET /api/v1/attestation/verdicts/latest?device=` returns its newest verdict (`attestation_verdicts:get`), and `POST /api/v1/attestation/verdicts` with `{"device_id": ...}` re-attests it now (`attestation_verdicts:create`). Every verb is bound to the caller's tenant. A server without a verdict store answers 503.
