- **CI: toolchain-image stages no longer run out of /tmp** — `scripts/in-toolchain-image.sh` takes `CI_WORK_VOLUME=on`, which ci.yml sets for every job, and then mounts an anonymous volume at /work and points TMPDIR and GOTMPDIR at it. Before, they used the container's 3 GB /tmp tmpfs. `go test` keeps each package's work directory, including the coverage-instrumented archives, until it exits. On a tenth of the module (32 of 319 packages, race + atomic coverage, -p 2) that work directory peaked at 810 MB and test temp dirs at 267 MB. Across the whole module, the Devcontainer image job's `make coverage` and the Race Detector filled /tmp and failed with "No space left on device" (ruling 108, Company Agent m1311). The volume is removed with the container. Local runs keep /tmp.
