- **The permitted-authentication-method check is go-qrdb's (BREAKING).** `pkg/auth.CheckAuthMethod`, `auth.MethodWebAuthn` and `auth.AuthMethodNotPermittedError` are removed. The mTLS authenticator, the PIV login and the WebAuthn account-standing check call `qrdbsdk.CheckAuthMethod`, the function qrdb's own middleware runs, and a refusal is `*qrdbsdk.AuthMethodNotPermittedError`. Stored policy entries are now compared in qrdb's canonical form (trimmed and lowercased) rather than as stored, so an entry written with different case or surrounding space now matches as it does on qrdb's planes.
