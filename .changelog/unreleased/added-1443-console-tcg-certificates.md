- **Web console TCG Certificates screen.** `#/ca/tcg` issues EK and AK certificates from a PEM or Base64 public key and signs a TCG CSR into IAK and IDevID certificates through the CA's `/api/v1/ca/tcg/ek`, `/ca/tcg/ak` and `/ca/tcg/sign-csr` endpoints, with a download for each result. The nav entry shows only where the CA is wired and the identity holds `certs:issue`. Device enrollment is not offered in the console: its response carries the credential-activation secret.
