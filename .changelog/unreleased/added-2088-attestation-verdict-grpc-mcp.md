- **Continuous attestation verdicts on gRPC, Unix and MCP.** The verdict verbs are now served beyond REST. The gRPC and Unix-socket RPCs are `ListAttestationVerdicts` (`attestation_verdicts:list`), `GetLatestAttestationVerdict` (`attestation_verdicts:get`) and `ReattestDevice` (`attestation_verdicts:create`). The MCP methods `attestation.verdict.list`, `attestation.verdict.latest` and `attestation.verdict.reattest` use the same pairs. The gRPC, Unix and MCP client transports call them too. Every verb is bound to the caller's tenant. A server without a verdict store answers Unavailable.
