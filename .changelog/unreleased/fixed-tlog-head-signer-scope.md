- **Security: the CA checkpoint signer refuses any log but the CA's** — `tlog.HeadSignerProvider` is bound to the CA log's tenant, database and log name and refuses every other log with `tlog.ErrLogOutOfScope`, so no other log on the embedded node can have its heads signed by the CA key. A reopened CA log must verify under the configured origin and CA key, or start-up fails with `tlog.ErrExistingLogMismatch`. Release builds of xkmsd now carry `qrdb_vlog`.
