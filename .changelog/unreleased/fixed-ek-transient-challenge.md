- **`AnswerCredentialChallenge` works without a persistent EK** -- when the EK certificate was in NV but no EK was loaded at the configured persistent handle (the state `TPM2_Clear` leaves behind), the challenge was answered against an unloaded handle and `TPM2_ActivateCredential` failed. The EK is now created transiently from the TCG EK Credential Profile low-range template matching the certificate (RSA-2048 or ECC P-256), checked against the certificate's public key, used, and flushed; the persistent EK is never created or re-persisted. New typed errors: `ErrEKCertNotInNV`, `ErrEKTemplateUnsupported` (including high-range certificates) and `ErrEKPublicKeyMismatch`. File: `pkg/tpm2/activation_key.go`.
