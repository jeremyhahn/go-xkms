- **The device-attest-01 client now answers with the TPM2_Certify evidence the `tpm` verifier accepts, and signs its CSR with the certified key** (#1059). After #789 the verifier requires WebAuthn Level 2 §8.3 evidence, but the client still sent a TPM2_Quote of PCR state, which the verifier refuses with `ErrCertifyWrongType`. It also signed the finalize CSR with the ACME account key, so even valid evidence could not have bound to it.
  - `deviceattest01.Setup(keyAuth, tpm, key, backend)` now has the IAK certify `key`, the TPM-resident certificate key, over `TPMExtraData(alg, keyAuth)`. `alg` is the IAK's own signature algorithm (PS256 for the default IAK), and `x5c` is the IAK certificate the TPM holds. `SetupWithPCRs` and the `Quote`, `KeyAttributes`, `TPMAttributes` and `TPM2BPublic` client interfaces are removed. `TrustedPlatformModule` is now the `IAKAttributes`/`IAKCertificate`/`CertifyKey` subset of `pkg/tpm2.TrustedPlatformModule`, so any go-xkms TPM satisfies it.
  - `acmeclient.TPMModule.Quote` and `TPMQuote` are replaced by `CreateAttestedKey(name, keyAuth)`. It creates a P-256 key named by the certificate's common name under the Platform SRK on first use, reuses it afterwards, and returns the challenge response and the key's `crypto.Signer`. `NewXKMSTPMAdapter` now takes the TPM key-blob store. `Client.DeviceAttest` drops its PCR-selection argument. It signs the CSR with the attested key and names the order's permanent identifier in the RFC 4043 `permanentIdentifier` otherName that finalize matches. `startAttestServer` serves the response verbatim instead of wrapping it a second time.
  - Files: `pkg/acme/challenges/device-attest-01/{setup,deviceattest01}.go`, `pkg/acme/client/{tpm,enrollment}.go`, `pkg/acme/client/service/{device_manager,device_tpm}.go`, `pkg/trust/clusterca/acme.go`.
  - Verified by `pkg/acme/challenges/device-attest-01/setup_simulator_test.go` and `pkg/acme/client/tpm_simulator_test.go`. Both use the in-process simulator with RSA-PSS and ECDSA IAKs. The server's `VerifyAttestation` and `BindCSRKey` accept the client's evidence and the CSR signed by the certified key. They refuse a software key, another Platform-SRK key, the account key, and a certification presented under another AIK's certificate.
