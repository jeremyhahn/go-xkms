- **Web console PIV Slots view.** A new Security screen at `#/piv/slots` (shown with `piv:read`) lists the PIV slots with key and certificate presence, subject, algorithm and expiry; it shows and exports a slot certificate as PEM, installs a pasted PEM certificate, deletes a certificate after a confirm, and makes a CSR signed by the slot key, which never leaves the card (go-xkms#1443).
