- **A Tenant Admin could read, delete, initialize, unseal and inspect another tenant's barrier over gRPC, and change any tenant's entitlements and quotas, including its own plan, over every transport. A tenant named in a request is now bound to the caller once, in `XKMSService`, and plan changes need the dedicated qrdb permissions plus a platform identity.** `auth.Authorizer.Authorize` binds the caller's *own* tenant as the request's target tenant (`pkg/auth/authorize.go`). A Tenant Admin's self-scoped `tenants:*` and `tenant_barrier:*` grants therefore passed the gate whatever tenant the request named. REST and MCP each re-checked in their handlers. gRPC's `GetTenant`, `DeleteTenant`, `TenantBarrierInit`, `TenantBarrierUnseal` and `TenantBarrierStatus` did not (go-xkms#1112). The check now lives in one place. `pkg/xkms/tenant_binding.go` adds `bindTenantTarget`, which admits a cross-tenant identity (`auth.IsCrossTenant`) for any tenant and a tenant-scoped identity only for its own, and `requirePlatformScope`. Both refuse with `*xkms.TenantBindingError`, which unwraps to `qrdbsdk.ErrTenantMissing` and renders its message. That is exactly what go-qrdb's TenantService returns for an unknown tenant, so on every transport a foreign tenant ID gets the same status and body as an unused one. The binding guards `GetTenant`, `DeleteTenant`, `TenantBarrierInit`, `TenantBarrierUnseal` and `TenantBarrierStatus`. `ListTenants` now filters to the caller's tenant in the service; gRPC previously listed every tenant to a tenant-scoped caller. REST's `isCrossTenantAccess` and MCP's `refuseCrossTenantAdmin` / `tenantAdminAccessDenied` are deleted, as are REST's and MCP's list filters and the gRPC barrier-registry fallback in `GetTenant`, `ListTenants` and `DeleteTenant`, which bypassed the servicer. Those RPCs now answer `Unavailable` when no `XKMSService` is wired. The same audit found two more caller-supplied tenant targets, now bound the same way. `BarrierEnableEncryption` let a caller holding `seal:enable_encryption` migrate another tenant's barrier, or the system barrier with an empty `TenantID`. `SignCSR`, over REST and MCP, stamped a caller-supplied tenant into the issued certificate's SPIFFE SAN. Entitlements and quotas were gated on `tenants:update` (go-xkms#1113). They now require go-qrdb's `tenants:set_entitlements` and `tenants:set_quotas` on REST (`routes.go`), MCP (`authz.go`) and gRPC (`s.authorize` and the `qrdb.v1.permission` options in `xkms.proto`, regenerated). The service admits only a platform identity, so a Tenant Admin cannot change even its own plan. That service check is needed because both permissions are self-scoped in go-qrdb and its built-in Admin role grants `tenants:set_entitlements`. No built-in role grants `tenants:set_quotas`, so changing quotas now needs a custom role granting it to a system-tenant identity. Three more enumeration oracles are gone. The REST and MCP handler checks answered "qrdb/seal: tenant not found" where a genuine miss answered "tenant: not found". gRPC and MCP answered a genuine miss with an internal error: `qrdbsdk.ErrTenantMissing` is now mapped to not-found in `tenantErrorToGRPC`, MCP's `tenantSentinelCodes`, `apierrors.BarrierSentinelClasses`, REST's `handleBarrierError` and `caErrorStatusMap`, and MCP's `mapCAError`. gRPC `DeriveKey` now passes the caller's tenant, which it omitted, so a stored-key derivation resolves the tenant-scoped backend rather than the unscoped one. New actions are `auth.ActionSetEntitlements` and `auth.ActionSetQuotas`, registered in `internal/authzscan`. Verified by `pkg/xkms/tenant_binding_test.go`, which runs against go-qrdb's real TenantService over an in-memory barrier: tenant A → B is refused as not-found for get, delete, barrier init, unseal and status and for both plan changes; the refusal equals a genuine miss; A → A is allowed except for plan changes; the system identity reaches every tenant; the list is filtered; and the enable-encryption and CSR targets are bound. Per-transport not-found mapping is covered by `TestTenantHandlers_CrossTenantRefusalMatchesGenuineMiss` and `TestTenantHandlers_PlanChangeRefusedForTenantScope` (REST), `TestTenantRPCs_CrossTenantBinding` (gRPC), `TestHandleGetTenant_CrossTenantRefusalMatchesGenuineMiss` and `TestHandleUpdateTenantPlan_TenantScopedRefused` (MCP), and by the updated authorization golden tables on all three transports (go-xkms#1112, go-xkms#1113).
