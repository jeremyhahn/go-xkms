- **Ceremony holder certificates name the registered holder, never the CSR (go-qrdb v0.28 contract).** Officer, custodian and `init sign-csr` certificates signed by the init ceremony and by the ACME admin plane's ceremony are now `CN=<registered username>`, `OU=<scope tenant>`, `OU=<profile>` (one RDN each), with no subject alternative name and only the CSR's key; previously the CSR's subject and names were copied, so registering an officer with a CSR for `CN=admin, OU=system` minted a login as the system tenant's admin. `pkg/init.CAAdapter.SignCSR` and the ACME admin adapter pass go-qrdb's `SignCSROptions.RawSubject` through the new `ca.SignOptions.RawSubject` (`TrustedCASignOptions.RawSubject` in `sdk/go`), which the CA writes verbatim while copying none of the CSR's names; combining it with another source of names is `ca.ErrRawSubjectWithNames`, a malformed one `ca.ErrInvalidRawSubject` (`ErrCARawSubjectWithNames`, `ErrCAInvalidRawSubject`). No SPIFFE SAN is stamped on a holder certificate any more (go-qrdb refuses one); the SO certificate the CA issues keeps it. The adapters also stop forwarding go-qrdb's holder label as an xkms CA profile name, which a real CA refused with `ca: profile not found`. Nil sign options are refused with `ErrNilSignOptions`.
