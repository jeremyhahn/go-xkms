- **Device security-event record binding (#1817)** — owner ruling 7dbbb72 item 181 (b): `securityevent.DetachedDigest` builds the RFC 9711 §4.2.18.2 Detached-Submodule-Digest (`[alg, digest]`, SHA-256/384/512 by COSE integer or name) over the record's Claims-Set, and `securityevent.VerifyDetached` checks a detached record against the digest from verified device-signed evidence in constant time before decoding it, refusing a mismatch (`ErrDigestMismatch`), a malformed array (`ErrMalformedDigest`) or another algorithm (`ErrUnsupportedAlgorithm`).
