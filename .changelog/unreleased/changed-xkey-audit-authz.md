- **xkey authorizes audit reads over go-qrdb RBAC instead of uid 0** (go-xkms#1605): `AdminService.GetAuditLogs` and `ExportAuditLogs` ask the go-qrdb authorizer set with `SetAuthorizer` whether the go-qrdb identity bound to the request context holds `audit:read`. A missing authorizer, a missing identity or a denial refuses with `*AuditReadDeniedError`, which matches `ErrAdminNotAuthorized` and unwraps to the authorizer's denial. The uid-0 `IsAdmin` and its Wails binding are removed. `sdk/go` re-exports go-qrdb's `Authorizer`, `Identity`, `PermissionAuditRead` and the identity and permission helpers as `QRDB*`. SPEC: authz-xkey-uid0.
