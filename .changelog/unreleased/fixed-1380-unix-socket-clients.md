- **Every xkmsd unix-socket client dials gRPC over TLS with a client certificate** -- the socket is the qrdb node's TLS listener, which serves gRPC only, so the clients that still spoke plaintext or REST there failed. The Go SDK's unix transport carries its TCG calls over gRPC (credential submit and seal-strategy reads report not-supported on the socket), and `unix://` URLs passed to `NewFromURL` dial with TLS. The SDK SSH client and `xkmsctl ssh` present their client certificate on `unix://`. xkey reaches a `unix://` server, and the phone bridge reaches xkmsd, through the same TLS endpoint as its network transports. The bridge reads `xkmsd.tls_ca_file`, `xkmsd.tls_cert_file` and `xkmsd.tls_key_file`, and the CLI reads `XKMSD_TLS_CA`, `XKMSD_TLS_CERT` and `XKMSD_TLS_KEY`. `pkg/config`'s `CreateAuthenticator` refuses a disabled or empty auth type with `ErrAuthenticationDisabled` and accepts no `noop`/`none` type (go-xkms#1380).
