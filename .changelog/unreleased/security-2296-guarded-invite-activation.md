- **An invitation can no longer reinstate an account an administrator disabled during redemption.** Invite passkey activation is a qrdb update guarded pending-to-active (`UpdateUserRequest.Guard`), and PIV invite activation binds its certificate and activates in one conditional commit (`CertActivator.ActivateWithCertBinding`); an account that left pending after the invite's check is refused with nothing written. A node user service without `CertActivator`, or a plain qrdb user client, refuses invite activation rather than writing unguarded.
