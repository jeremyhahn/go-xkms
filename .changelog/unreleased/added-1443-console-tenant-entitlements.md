- **Web console Tenant Entitlements view.** `#/tenants/entitlements` (Settings section, shown where tenants are wired and the identity holds `tenants:set_entitlements`) sets which isolation modes, barrier modes, dedicated Raft, volume storage, system-managed barrier and dedicated WAL a tenant may use. It sends the whole entitlement set through `PUT /api/v1/tenants/{tenantID}/entitlements` and shows the entitlements the server returns.
