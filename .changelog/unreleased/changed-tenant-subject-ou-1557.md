- **Every issued certificate carries its tenant as the first subject OU.** `ca.StampTenant` sets `Subject.OrganizationalUnit[0]` to the request's tenant on every issuance path: `TrustedCA` `SignCSR`/`IssueCertificate*`, TCG EK/AK/IAK/IDevID enrollment, the ACME finalize paths and the `pkg/acme/issuance` pipeline, the init ceremony and first-boot admin enrollment. An OU the CSR carries is replaced, not appended to. The tenant is the authenticated caller's (xkms servicers), the ACME account's, or `system` for the node's own certificates, and `qrdbsdk.TenantFromClientCert` reads it back when no SPIFFE tenant SAN is present. A request without a tenant is refused with `ca.ErrTenantRequired`. Breaking: `SignOptions.TenantID`, `CertificateRequest.TenantID` and `issuance.Request.TenantID` are now required, and `SignCSRRaw`, `IssueEKCertificateRaw`, `IssueAKCertificateRaw`, `SignTCGCSRIDevIDRaw` and `EnrollDeviceRaw` take a trailing tenant. CA hierarchy certificates, the TCG LAK and FIDO2 attestation certificates are not stamped. SPEC: tenant-subject-ou.
