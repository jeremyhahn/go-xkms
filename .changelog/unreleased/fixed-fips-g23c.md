- **The Go SDK re-exports the Noise FIPS refusal.** `sdk/go/bootstrap` now carries `ErrNoiseFIPSUnsupported`, the typed error every Noise key and session entry point returns under `GODEBUG=fips140=only`, so a consumer recognises the X25519 refusal with `errors.Is` without importing `pkg/trust/bootstrap`. The `sdk/go/bootstrap` Noise composition test and the `sdk/go/client/ssh` session-dial tests on every transport assert the refusal (a typed `*session.HandshakeError` for the curve25519 key exchange) under `fips140=only` and the round trip under `fips140=off` (part of #23).
