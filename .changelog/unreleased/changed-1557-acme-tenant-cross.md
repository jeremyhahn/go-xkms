- **ACME admin tenant lifecycle routes require qrdb's cross-scope tenant grants to reach another tenant** -- on the ACME admin plane, `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/tenants/{id}` and `PUT /api/v1/tenants/{id}/status` need the self-scoped `tenants:*` grant on the caller's own tenant and `tenants:{read,update,delete}:cross` (`qrdbsdk.PermissionTenants*Cross`) on any other; `GET /api/v1/tenants` needs `tenants:list` from a tenant-scoped caller (which lists only its own tenant) and `tenants:list:cross` from a system-tenant caller; `POST /api/v1/tenants` needs `tenants:create:cross`, and `CreateTenant` no longer re-checks platform scope by hand. A system-tenant caller without the cross-scope grant is refused; a tenant-scoped caller naming another tenant is answered `404 Tenant not found`, as for a missing tenant (go-xkms#1557, go-xkms#1225).
