- **ACME admin tenant lifecycle routes require qrdb's cross-scope tenant grants** -- `GET`/`POST /api/v1/tenants`, `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/tenants/{id}` and `PUT /api/v1/tenants/{id}/status` on the ACME admin plane are gated on `tenants:{list,create,read,update,delete}:cross` (`qrdbsdk.PermissionTenants*Cross`) instead of the self-scoped `tenants:*`, and `CreateTenant` no longer re-checks platform scope by hand. Only a system-tenant caller holding the grant (the platform Admin) reaches them; a tenant-scoped Auditor or Admin that could read its own tenant record there is now refused (go-xkms#1557).
