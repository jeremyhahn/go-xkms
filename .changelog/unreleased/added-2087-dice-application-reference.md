- **`dice.Verify` can hold a device to its registered application.** `VerifyOptions.ApplicationReference` (32 bytes) makes `Verify` read the alias certificate's measurements claim (273) and refuse a chain whose application evidence differs or is missing (`ErrApplicationEvidenceMismatch`); a reference of any other length is `ErrInvalidApplicationReference`. The 273 shape reader moved from device-attest-01 to `pkg/attestation/dice` as `ApplicationEvidence` and `ErrMeasurementsShape`; `ErrPossessionMeasurementsShape` still matches every shape refusal.
