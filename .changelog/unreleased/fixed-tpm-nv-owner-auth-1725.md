- **TPM certificate NV writes on an owned TPM (#1725)** — `ProvisionIDevIDCert`, `ProvisionIAKCert` and `ProvisionLAKCert` take the owner hierarchy authorization (the SO PIN) and define and write their NV index under it (TPM 2.0 Part 3 §31.3.1, §31.7.1), instead of an empty password that failed with `TPM_RC_BAD_AUTH`. Provisioning again rewrites a matching index instead of failing with `TPM_RC_NV_DEFINED`, and an index with other attributes is refused with `ErrNVCertIndexMismatch`. The EK certificate is defined under the owner hierarchy too, at the index its key type names (TCG EK Credential Profile v2.1 §2.2.1.4: ECC P-256 at `0x01C0000A`, not the RSA `0x01C00002`). `sdk/go` re-exports `ErrTPMNVCertTooLarge`, `ErrTPMNVCertIndexMismatch`, `ErrTPMNVCertHierarchyAuth` and `ErrTPMEKCertKeyType`.
