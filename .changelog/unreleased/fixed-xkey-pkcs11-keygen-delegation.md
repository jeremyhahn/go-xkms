- **`C_GenerateKey` through xkey now creates AES and generic secret keys, and xkey's PKCS#11 export no longer releases a sensitive key** -- Before this fix, `EmbeddedTransport.GenerateKey` had its own RSA/ECDSA/Ed25519 switch, so every secret-key request from the PKCS#11 module (`Algorithm: "Symmetric"`) failed. Separately, its local `ExportKeyMaterial` handed out keys marked `CKA_SENSITIVE`. Now every `EmbeddedTransport` key operation (generate, sign, verify, encrypt, decrypt, derive, ECDH, wrap, unwrap, export, import parameters, import) is one call to the matching xkms service method. Secret keys of 128, 192 or 256 bits generate in-process, over the xkey socket and on `xkey usb device start`. The service's own key templates and its refusal of sensitive and non-exportable keys apply, and an import ceremony can now be negotiated for an AES key. Errors map the same way for every operation: a missing key or backend is `ErrKeyNotFound` / `ErrBackendNotFound`, and any other failure is the operation's sentinel, including the new `ErrKeyGenerationFailed`. `ErrImportTemplateUnsupported`, `MessageError` and `WrappedError` are removed. Files: `xkey/pkg/pkcs11/service.go`, `errors.go`. Docs: `xkey/docs/pkcs11/engineering.md`, `architecture.md`.
