- **An unknown `datastore.engine` is refused before it reaches go-qrdb** -- `datastore.Open` and `xkmsd init` open the embedded node without `config.Validate`, so a typo such as `engine: carrier-pigeon` went to go-qrdb unchecked. `embeddednode.OpenEmbeddedNode` now checks the engine first. `config.Validate` and that check share one list (go-qrdb's SDK backends `memory`, `file`, `pebble`) and return the typed `*config.ErrUnknownDatastoreEngine` / `*config.ErrUnknownDatastoreMode`, naming the bad value and the accepted ones, in place of an untyped message. `engine` and `mode` are trimmed and lowercased before the check, and `Validate` stores the normalised values, so `" Pebble "` is accepted everywhere instead of being refused by `Validate` but accepted by the node. Files: `pkg/config/datastore.go`, `pkg/config/config.go`, `pkg/embeddednode/embedded_node.go`.
