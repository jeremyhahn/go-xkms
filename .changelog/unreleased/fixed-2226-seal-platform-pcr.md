- **tpm2 `Seal` under the platform policy binds every requested PCR.** A sealed object with `TPMPolicy: platform` and a `TPMAttributes.PCRSelection` is now bound to the union of the platform PCR set and the requested selection. Before, it was bound to the platform set only, so a requested PCR outside it (PCR 11 when the platform set is {7}) was silently left out and extending it did not close the object. Unseal rebuilds the same PolicyPCR session from the recorded selection. A selection in a bank other than the platform bank is refused.
