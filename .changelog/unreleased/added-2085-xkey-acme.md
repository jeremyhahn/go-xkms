- **xkey ACME verbs in CLI and GUI (#2085)**: `xkey acme account`, `revoke`, `renew`, `enroll` and `endorse`, and the same five verbs in the desktop GUI through the Wails-bound `services.ACMEService` and the ACME view's Device enrollment card, so a user can register and manage an ACME account, revoke and renew certificates, and enroll or endorse a device from either front end. Both are thin callers of `pkg/acme/client` through `sdk/go` with no ACME logic of their own: `services.RunACMEEnrollment` is the one enroll-01 / endorse-01 / device-attest-01 challenge dispatch the CLI and GUI share, over the shared TPM (`SetTPMSource`, `NewACMETPMSource`), and renewal answers http-01 with a solver bound to a configurable listen address. In `pkg/acme/client/client.go` the account registration an order triggers now runs under the order's context, so its deadline also bounds the newAccount request. Tests: `xkey/cmd/xkey/cmd/acme_*_test.go`, `xkey/pkg/gui/services/acme_*_test.go`, `pkg/acme/client/register_account_ctx_test.go` and the frontend `xkey/frontend/src/views/ACME.test.ts`.
