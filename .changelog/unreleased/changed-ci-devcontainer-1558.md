- **CI runs every stage in the devcontainer image.** The devcontainer moves to `build/docker/devcontainer/`. Its staged Dockerfile builds a toolchain image, with every tool pinned in `scripts/tool-pins.sh`, and an integration image (`make docker-build`, `make toolchain-image`). `.github/workflows/ci.yml` follows the company ci-template v1.18, and each job runs its commands in the toolchain image through `scripts/in-toolchain-image.sh`. On every PR, CI runs lint, static analysis, `make build`, `make test` with the 90% coverage gate, `make security`, `make deps-check`, trivy on the integration and CLI images, the backend and acme-client GUI builds, the nested-module builds and the architecture gates. The full tier runs on a push to `main`, on `workflow_dispatch`, or on a PR labelled `ci:full`. It covers the nested-module tests, the raft tag matrix, the race detector, the TPM2 simulator and `make integration-test`. `scripts/reap-stale-ci-stacks.sh` removes compose stacks left behind by dead runners. `make deps-check` fails when a direct dependency is behind its newest release. `make docker-build` passes the caller's `GOPROXY` to the image's `go install` and `go mod download` stages, so CI builds the image through its own module proxy. The docs-only and train scoping (`pr-scope.sh`, `changed-go-packages.sh`) is removed. SPEC: release criteria (CI standard).
