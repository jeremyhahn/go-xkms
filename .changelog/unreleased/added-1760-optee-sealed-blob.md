- **OP-TEE TA sealed-blob format (#1760, ruling 251 item 177 (a))** — the blob trusted-firmware's TA returns from `SEAL` and accepts in `UNSEAL`: a tagged COSE_Encrypt0 (RFC 9052 §5.2) under A256GCM (RFC 9053 §4.1) with a random 96-bit nonce, the version in the protected header and the TA UUID as external_aad, keyed by HKDF-SHA-256 (RFC 5869) from the TA's hardware unique key. The codec is `pkg/enclaveprovider/opteeblob` (`DeriveKey`, `Seal`, `Unseal`, typed errors), with test vectors from QEMU's test HUK in `testdata/vectors.json` and the normative format in `docs/optee-sealed-blob/README.md`; RFC 9052 and RFC 5869 join `docs/references/`.
