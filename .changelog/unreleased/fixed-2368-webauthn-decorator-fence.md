- **A refused or crashed first WebAuthn enrolment leaves no decorator side effect.** A credential-store decorator (a composer's auth factor) saw an account's first credential before its first-credential claim committed, so a ceremony fenced out at Commit, or one that crashed after its fenced save, left an authentication factor behind; the relying party now persists the record through the claim and calls the decorated Save only after the claim commits, deleting the credential through the decorator if that Save fails.
