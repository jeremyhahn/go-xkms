- **Withdrawing the last trust root refuses verification, and the xkmsd peer gate reads the trust-root store.** A verifier backed by the store whose last root of its chain purpose is quarantined or removed now returns `ErrNoTrustAnchors` from `Verify` and `VerifyChain` instead of passing a structural-only check. The xkmsd attested-caller gate takes the anchors its policy leaves unset from the store's `idevid-issuer` roots on every peer, so importing, quarantining or removing one takes effect at once. The `yubikey-attestation`, `apple-attestation` and `dice-attestation` purposes are withdrawn: they are not in the trust-roots taxonomy, and those formats anchor on their configured roots.
