- **Web console Rotation Policy view (#1443)** — `#/keys/rotation-policy` shows, sets and removes a key's automatic rotation policy (enabled, interval in hours or days, versions kept, auto rotate, the server-computed next rotation) through GET, PUT and DELETE `/api/v1/keys/{id}/rotation-policy`. A typed helper, `web/app/src/lib/keyRotationPolicy.ts`, calls the routes over the SDK's HTTP core. The nav entry sits under Keys & Crypto and shows only where the identity holds `keys:get`.
