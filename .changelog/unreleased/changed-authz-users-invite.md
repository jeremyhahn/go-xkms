- **Invitations are minted only under `users:invite`.** `CreateUser` now asks go-qrdb's `users:invite` before it creates an email-login-key account and mints its activation invitation; a caller holding `users:create` alone is refused with `PermissionDeniedError` and no account is created. Accounts created without an email login key (bootstrap, no invitation) still need only `users:create`.
