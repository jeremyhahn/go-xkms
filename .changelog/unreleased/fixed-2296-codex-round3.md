- **A crash inside a TPM key pair recovery no longer leaves an apparently complete mismatched pair.** A create-only `SaveKeyPair` that replaces an incomplete pair now claims the public-blob slot with a recovery fence, overwrites the private blob, and publishes the real public blob last; `LoadKeyPair` reads the public blob first and reports a fenced pair as incomplete (`ErrBlobNotFound`), and the next create-only save takes the fence over and finishes the pair.
- **Invitation passkey enrolment keeps a credential store decorator's side effects.** The first credential of an invited account is saved through the configured `CredentialStore.Save` with the first-credential claim in the context (`webauthn.WithFirstCredentialClaim`), so a composer's decorator (its qrdb fido2 factor) sees it while the claim still fences the credential until commit.
