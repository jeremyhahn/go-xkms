- **Web console Tenant TPM screen (`#/tenants/tpm`).** Under Identity & Access, an operator picks a tenant, sees its TPM mode (none, shared or vtpm) and changes it after a confirm that warns the server refuses the change while the tenant holds TPM keys or policies; for a vtpm tenant the screen shows the virtual TPM's state, PID, socket and restarts and starts or stops it (stop asks first). The entry shows only when the deployment wires TPM policies and the identity holds `tenants:read`.
