- **A stop-on-first phone scan no longer leaks its scan goroutine** -- `(*BLETransport).ScanWithOptions` with stopOnFirst signalled each new xKey device on a one-slot channel with a blocking send while holding its result lock. When several distinct xKey phones advertised, a later callback blocked forever, leaking the scan goroutine and, on adapters whose StopScan waits for the callback, hanging the scan itself. The signal is now non-blocking; devices heard before the stop are still returned. Files: `xkey/pkg/phone/ble_transport.go`.
