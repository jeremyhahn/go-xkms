- **The FIDO2 integration targets compile in the DAO codecs.** `integration-test-fido2` and its `-cli`, `-webauthn`, `-multiprotocol` and `-hardware` siblings now test `./test/integration/fido2/...` with `codec_cbor codec_json codec_msgpack`, as the other integration targets do. Without them, the WebAuthn credential store that `webauthntest` builds could not create its `json` codec, and the virtual-authenticator registration and authentication ceremonies failed before they started.
