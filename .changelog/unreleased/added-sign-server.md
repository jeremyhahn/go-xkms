- **An embeddable, node-local, sign-only xkms server.** The new `pkg/signserver`, re-exported as `sdk/go/signserver` and buildable with `CGO_ENABLED=0`, serves a remote-mode PKCS#11 module on a unix socket behind three gates: an exclusive SO_PEERCRED uid/gid allowlist, an RPC allowlist (Health, Sign, token-record reads and PIN-lockout counter writes), and `Sign` only for the keys a slot names by `CKA_LABEL`. `CreateKey` creates those keys idempotently through the module itself. `pkg/api/unix` gains `GRPCConfig.ExclusivePeers` (`NewExclusivePeerCredentialPolicy`, `ErrNoAdmittedPeers`) and caller-supplied unary and stream interceptors.
