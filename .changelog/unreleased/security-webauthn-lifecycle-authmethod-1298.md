- **WebAuthn login ignored the account lifecycle and the permitted-auth-method policy: a suspended, disabled or locked account, or one whose `AuthMethods` excluded `webauthn`, still logged in with its passkey** (#1298). The qrdb user store's `materialize` (`pkg/webauthn/qrdb_userstore.go`) dropped the record's `Lifecycle` and `AuthMethods`, and no login path checked them, while the mTLS authenticator did. `webauthn.User` now carries `Lifecycle()` and `AuthMethods()`; the qrdb store copies both from the record and `DefaultUser` starts `active` with no restriction (`SetLifecycle`, `SetAuthMethods`). The new `webauthn.CheckAccountStanding` refuses any lifecycle that cannot authenticate (only `qrdbsdk.LifecycleActive` can, so an empty or unknown state is refused) with `*AccountNotActiveError`, and applies the permitted-method check the mTLS authenticator uses, now exported as `auth.CheckAuthMethod` with `auth.MethodWebAuthn`. The service applies it at every login:
  - **Begin:** `BeginLoginForEmail`/`BeginLoginForHandle` answer an account that may not log in with the decoy an unknown account gets (#1296); `BeginLogin(userID)` answers it with `ErrUserNotFound`, exactly as for an unknown handle; `pkg/auth_factors`' `BeginChallenge` treats a hint naming it as an unknown hint.
  - **Finish:** `finishLoginUser`, behind `FinishLogin`/`FinishLoginPair` and therefore the browser route, the SDK REST routes, gRPC `FinishAuthentication`, the discoverable flow and the qrdb FIDO2 factor's `Verify`, refuses a valid assertion with a bare `ErrVerificationFailed` (`validate login: verification failed`). The account's state never reaches the client; it is logged at Warn ("ceremony refused for account not in good standing").
  - **Enrolment:** registration begin (including the zero-credential onboarding path), `BeginAddCredential` and registration finish refuse an account that is not active with `ErrRegistrationRefused`, the same 403 / `PermissionDenied` as every other refused enrolment (#1294, #1296).
  - The browser route (`pkg/webauthn/http`) now maps a verification failure to 401 `verification_failed` before `ErrUserNotFound`. A finish against a decoy session wraps the decoy handle's not-found, and was answered 404 `user_not_found`, unlike the 401 a wrong assertion against a real account gets.
