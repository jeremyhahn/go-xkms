- **WebAuthn credentials are go-qrdb DAO entities on the injected factory** -- `pkg/webauthn` has one credential store, built by `NewQRDBUserStoreAdapter` from its new required `DAOFactory` option (entity type `webauthn_credentials`, default database); users stay go-qrdb's, and session state is unchanged. `BackendUserStore`, `BackendCredentialStore`, `MemoryUserStore` and `MemoryCredentialStore` are gone, along with `rest.WebAuthnStores`/`WebAuthnStoresConfig`; the adapter no longer needs a guarded KV client. `rest.NewServer` refuses a WebAuthn config without a pre-built service (`ErrWebAuthnServiceRequired`); `rest.HostConfig` and `rest.WebAuthnServiceParams` take a `DAOFactory`, which the API-module host and xkmsd supply from the datastore. Credentials stored by the old stores are not migrated.
