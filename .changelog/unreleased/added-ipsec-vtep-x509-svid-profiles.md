- **`ipsec-vtep` and `x509-svid` certificate profiles under name-constrained subordinate CAs.** `pkg/ca`, re-exported from `sdk/go`, adds two caller-named profiles. `ipsec-vtep` issues IKEv2 endpoint certificates (`id-kp-ipsecIKE`, IP SANs, 30-day default and 90-day maximum lifetime). `x509-svid` issues SPIFFE X.509-SVID leaves (one SPIFFE ID URI SAN, serverAuth and clientAuth, 1-hour default and 24-hour maximum). Both take their subject and names only from the issuing caller, never from the CSR. `(*CA).IssueSubordinateCA` certifies a caller-held key as an RFC 5280 name-constrained subordinate. `SignOptions.Validity` sets a lifetime shorter than a day. A pre-sign check, applied to every profile, refuses a leaf whose names violate the issuer's name constraints. The `tp-attestationEvidence` extension (OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.29377.101.23) carries the verdict ID, PCR digest and attestation time.
