- **A Verifier appraises a device's security-event record against the evidence the device signed over it (#1765)**. The optional backend capability `types.SecurityEventAttester` returns `AttestSecurityEvents(ctx, nonce)`: COSE_Sign1 EAT evidence carrying the Verifier's `eat_nonce` (RFC 9711 §4.1) and the record's Detached-Submodule-Digest (RFC 9711 §4.2.18.2), the signer's DICE chain, and the record beside them. `securityevent.Appraise` verifies the chain through `dice.Verify`, the ES256 signature, the nonce and the record's digest, rejecting each failure with its own typed error.
