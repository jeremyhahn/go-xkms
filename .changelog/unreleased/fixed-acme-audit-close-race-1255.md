- **`FileAuditLogger.Log` could panic with "send on closed channel" when it raced `Close`** (#1255). `Log` checked `l.closed.Load()` and then sent on `l.buffer` as two separate, unsynchronized steps; `Close` did a compare-and-swap on `closed` and then `close(l.buffer)`. A `Log` call that passed the closed check just before `Close` ran could still be sending on `l.buffer` after `Close` closed it. `FileAuditLogger` now carries a `closeMu sync.RWMutex`: `Log` holds the read lock across its closed check and its send, including the synchronous `writeEvent` fallback used when the buffer is full, and `Close` takes the write lock before the compare-and-swap and the channel close. The two can no longer interleave. Every event `Log` accepted before `Close` began is still drained and flushed by the processor goroutine before `Close` returns; a `Log` call after `Close` has completed still returns `ErrLoggerClosed`.
  - Files: `pkg/acme/audit/logger.go`, `pkg/acme/audit/logger_test.go`.
  - Verified by `TestFileAuditLogger_LogCloseRace` (50 goroutines logging concurrently with `Close`, confirmed to panic reliably under `-race` on the pre-fix code), `TestFileAuditLogger_CloseFlushesBufferedEvents` (25 buffered events all reach disk across `Close` with no intervening `Flush`), and `TestFileAuditLogger_LogAfterClose` (`Log` after `Close` returns `ErrLoggerClosed`). `go vet` and `go test -race -count=3` clean under `codec_cbor codec_json codec_msgpack tpm_simulator frost`.
  - Fixes #1255.
