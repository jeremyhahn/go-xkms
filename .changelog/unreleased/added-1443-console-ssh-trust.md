- **Web console SSH Trust view (#1443)** — a Security screen at `#/ssh/trust` (capability `ssh`, permission `ssh:read`) that lists, pins, verifies and removes known-host pins and adds, verifies and removes authorized_keys entries for a scope and owner through the `/xkms/v1/ssh` known-hosts and authorized-keys verbs; a mismatched host-key verify (409) shows the pinned and offered fingerprints.
