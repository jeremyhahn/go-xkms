- **The signer store persists as go-qrdb DAO records** -- `pkg/signing/store`'s `NewSignerStore(logger, daos)` takes the injected go-qrdb DAO factory instead of a `storage.Backend` and keeps signers and signature records in the `signing` database (`signers` and `signatures` entity types), with IDs derived from the CN and signature key and lookups by those natural keys. A nil factory is refused with `ErrNilDAOFactory`, and a factory that cannot open the database with `*StoreOpenError`. `xkms.New` builds the factory over `BackendConfig.CertStorage`, so signatures still live beside the certificates. The custom xxhash ID generator is gone. Records written by the old layout are not read.
