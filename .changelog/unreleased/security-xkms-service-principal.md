- **xkms's own calls on its embedded go-qrdb node run as a least-privilege service principal** -- every module runs on go-qrdb v0.46.0-alpha (which needs Go 1.27.1), whose embedded client refuses a call that carries no identity. `embeddednode.NewNode` now configures the node, standalone and clustered alike, with the service principal `xkms` (new leaf package `pkg/embeddednode/serviceprincipal`; audited as `qrdb-service:xkms`). Its grant is exactly `kv:get`, `kv:put`, `kv:delete`, `kv:list`, `kv:exists`, `lease:grant`, `lease:revoke`, `lease:keepalive` and `transaction:execute`, never a wildcard. The server's storage backend binds each call to `Node.ServiceContext` through the new `qrdbstorage.WithCallContext`. The OIDC credential refresh locks and the refresh scheduler's leadership lease do the same through the new `credential.WithLeaseContext`, wired by the new `compose.OIDCCredentialParams.LeaseContext`. A request-scoped call keeps the caller's identity and never runs as the principal. A remote datastore binds nothing, because its client already carries xkms's mTLS identity. With authentication enabled, xkmsd boots with a CA configured, provisions its JWT signing key and derives its composite-authentication methods again. A call the principal cannot bind fails with a typed error before it reaches the node.
