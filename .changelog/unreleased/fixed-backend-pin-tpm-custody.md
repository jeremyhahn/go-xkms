- **The `tpm2` backend PIN strategy leaves no copy of the PIN outside the TPM.** A PIN under `credentials.seal_strategy: barrier` is sealed to the TPM at its first release and its `credential://` credential is replaced by the TPM-sealed blob; later starts unseal the blob. A PIN that is not a `credential://` reference under barrier storage stops start-up with `BackendPINTPMCustodyError`, and `backend_pin_strategy` reports `tpm2` only once every PIN is in TPM custody. An active `rng.pkcs11.pin` beside `backends.pkcs11.pin` is resolved, held, sealed and zeroized on its own. `auto` detects a TPM 2.0 on the host even with `backends.tpm2` disabled. The backend PIN policy checks that need no secret (manual, storage, custody) run before config secret resolution, so a refused strategy never waits on a credential reference (#2262).
- **The backend PIN TPM custody migration is crash-safe.** The TPM-sealed blob is staged under `<name>-tpm2-staged` before the plaintext credential is deleted, and an interrupted migration is completed at the next start; default and `auto` strategies are refused before config secret resolution when the effective strategy cannot be served (#2262).
