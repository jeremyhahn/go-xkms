- **Trust roots are one persisted, purpose-classified store served as module verbs.** xkey's truststore moved with its history to `pkg/attestation/truststore` (`xkey/pkg/truststore` is gone; xkey imports the new path), and its `DAOStore` persists every root in the qrdb `trust_roots` database. `XKMSService` serves list, get, import, quarantine and remove on REST (`/api/v1/trust-roots`), gRPC and Unix (`KeystoreService.TrustRoot*`), QUIC, MCP (`trust.root.*`) and the embedded transport, behind the new system-scope `trust_roots` permissions granted to Admin and Owner. A quarantined (`synced`) root stays listed but anchors nothing, and re-importing a held certificate never changes its purpose. Docs: `docs/trust-roots/`. SPEC: trust-roots, rbac.
