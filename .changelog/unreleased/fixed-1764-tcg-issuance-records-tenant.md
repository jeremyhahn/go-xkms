CA: TCG device-identity issuance (EK, AK, IAK, IDevID, LAK) records the request's tenant when this CA signs, so `CertificateTenant` reports it and a tenant can revoke and list its own device certificates; before, every such certificate reported the system scope. A failed tenant record fails the issuance; a certificate signed with a caller-supplied Signer records none (pkg/ca/tcg.go `issueTCGCertificate`, #1764).
