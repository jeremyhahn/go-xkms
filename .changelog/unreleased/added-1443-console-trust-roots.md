- **The web console has a Trust Roots view.** `#/trust/roots` (Certificates & CA) lists the CA roots the server trusts, filtered by purpose, imports PEM bundles with a purpose and source, and quarantines or deletes a root after a confirm, over `/api/v1/trust-roots`. `GET /api/v1/auth/me` reports a new `trust_roots` capability flag, true when the trust-root handlers are wired; the nav entry shows only with that flag and `trust_roots:list`.
