# #2240: scheduled Devcontainer job must not burn hosted-runner hours

## Objective

Scheduled CI runs route the Devcontainer image job to ubuntu-latest (its
`runs-on` sends only main pushes and same-repo PRs to the self-hosted
`bigdisk-docker` pool), and `make coverage` runs for hours after a failed
`make build` because every step is gated on `!cancelled()` alone
(run 43874268242, 11-13 to 11-16).

## Slices (one commit each, .github/workflows/ci.yml only)

1. `ci:` Devcontainer job `runs-on`: `schedule` and `workflow_dispatch` on
   refs/heads/main also go to `["self-hosted","linux","x64","bigdisk-docker"]`.
   Fork PRs stay on ubuntu-latest. Test: actionlint.
2. `ci:` `make build` step gets `id: build`; `make coverage` adds
   `steps.build.outcome != 'failure'` to its `if:`. make security,
   deps-check and trivy keep their conditions. Test: actionlint.

## Out of scope

- The Integration and Soak jobs' `runs-on` (same expression; not in this brief).
- Any other workflow, secret, variable or action.
