# #2240: scheduled Devcontainer job must not burn hosted-runner hours

## Objective

Scheduled CI runs route the Devcontainer image job to ubuntu-latest (its
`runs-on` sends only main pushes and same-repo PRs to the self-hosted
`bigdisk-docker` pool), and `make coverage` runs for hours after a failed
`make build` because every step is gated on `!cancelled()` alone
(run 43874268242, 11-13 to 11-16).

## Slices (one commit each, .github/workflows/ci.yml only)

1. `ci:` Devcontainer job `runs-on`: `schedule` and `workflow_dispatch` on
   refs/heads/main also go to `["self-hosted","linux","x64","bigdisk-docker"]`.
   Fork PRs stay on ubuntu-latest. Test: actionlint.
2. `ci:` `make build` step gets `id: build`; `make coverage` adds
   `steps.build.outcome != 'failure'` to its `if:`. make security,
   deps-check and trivy keep their conditions. Test: actionlint.
3. `ci:` (ruling 740, see Rulings) Integration and Soak `runs-on` get the same schedule/workflow_dispatch-on-main clause. Test: actionlint.
4. `ci:` Codex review 5923170653 (PR #2241, 2026-11-16, at e8aceb376) P1 1-2:
   comment-only answers in 3305ee639 (HEAD). Test: actionlint, make lint.

## Rulings

- Ruling 740 (11-16) supersedes InfoSec W-1 / DS-8215 for schedule and
  workflow_dispatch on main only; pushes, same-repo PRs and fork PRs keep
  their routing. (Codex P1 1, ci.yml Integration and Soak comments.)

## Evidence

- Codex P1 2 (github.com has no bigdisk runner): github.com push run
  43481870662 on main (ec11e342b, 11-13) ran its Integration job on
  `self-hosted,linux,x64,bigdisk-docker` for about 2 h, so the pool exists
  there. The schedule guard's "no bigdisk runner" echo is about its run-count
  check, not runner availability. No github.server_url guard is added.

## Out of scope

- Any other workflow, secret, variable or action.
