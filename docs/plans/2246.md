# Plan: event-driven xkey OIDC command tests (#2246)

Red-main fix, ruling 742 item 4 (DS-9273), reshaped by ruling 777: the
tests must wait on events, with no wall-clock deadline that depends on
runner speed. Main 81a1a5fb3 is red on Nested Module Tests.

## Cause

In job 133717289375 the runner's storage syncs took 1.1-2.7 s. Three xkey
command tests used fixed 5 s and 10 s ceilings in `Eventually` and
`time.After` waits, and those ran out before the work finished:
`oidc_aws_cmds_test.go` (callback-server wait, mismatched-callback return,
`awsAutoRefreshUntil`), `oidc_barrier_test.go`
(`TestRunAutoRefresh_ReusesTheSameOpenBarrier_NotReopenedPerTick`) and
`oidc_login_flow_test.go` (printed-URL wait, login return).

## Slices

1. 34df2f0b3: the ceilings raised to a shared 60 s `slowStoreWait`.
2. 1fd5c698b (ruling 777): `slowStoreWait` replaced by event helpers in
   `testwait_test.go`: `eventLog` (goroutine-safe writer that closes a
   channel once the log holds the wanted complete line) and
   `eventCounter` (closes a channel on its N-th handler call). The waits
   block on those channels and on the loop's `done`; a loop that returns
   early fails at once. The state-mismatch test retries the dial until the
   listener accepts it. No `Eventually` or `time.After` remains in the
   three files; `go test -timeout` is the only hang guard. Refresh tick
   intervals (10 ms) and every assertion stay: no retry, no skip.

## Out of scope

Non-test code, the Makefile, workflows and the runner's storage.
