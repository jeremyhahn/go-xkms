## Follow-ups (rule 720)

- Each password-protected `Sign` loads the CA key a second time; a cached
  decrypted signer with a bounded lifetime would remove the cost.
- The scoped signer exposes only `Public` and `Sign`; callers needing the
  backend signer's other interfaces (e.g. `crypto.Decrypter`) get neither.
- `KeyAttributesFromConfig` leaks the passwords it opened when a later
  step (parent conversion) fails.
- `Load`, `createRootCertificate`, `createSingleIntermediateCertificate`
  leave their one-time key passwords uncleared (bounded, once per setup).
