# Plan: #2183 Codex round-4 P1 (4): WebAuthn first-credential claim recovery

## Objective

A crash between the first-credential claim and the credential save must not
leave an orphan claim that blocks every later first-credential registration
of the account (Tenant Agent ruling 952, Codex round 4 on PR #2183,
pkg/webauthn/credential_store.go:103).

go-qrdb offers per-entity atomic writes (SaveIfAbsent, CompareAndSwap,
DeleteIfMatch) but no multi-entity transaction, so the claim and the
credential cannot be written in one step. The fix is fenced orphan-claim
recovery:

- A claim is written pending, with a random token and a lease expiry.
- The winner saves the credential, then commits: CompareAndSwap from its own
  pending claim to committed. The CAS is the fence: a claimer whose claim was
  recovered loses the CAS, deletes the credential it saved, and is refused.
- A later claimer that finds a pending claim past its lease, while the
  account has no credential, takes it over with CompareAndSwap from the
  claim it read. A live claimer inside its lease is never taken over.
- A failed save releases with DeleteIfMatch of the claimer's own claim, so
  it cannot delete a claim another ceremony recovered.

## Slices

1. test: store-level regressions in credential_store_test.go: a crashed
   claim (never committed nor released) is recovered after its lease by a
   restarted store on the same factory; a live claim inside its lease still
   refuses; a slow claimer whose claim was recovered loses at commit and its
   credential is gone; an expired pending claim whose credential was saved
   is not recovered.
2. fix: credential_store.go claim entity + FirstCredentialClaim
   (Commit/Release), interfaces.go, the service.go caller and the test
   double in error_paths_test.go.

## Out of scope

The other three round-4 P1s (sibling lanes) and the add-a-passkey path.

## Round 5 (ruling 968): claim-bound save

A stale claimer that saves after its claim was recovered, then crashes
before Commit, must leave no usable credential (Codex review 5975930446).

- FirstCredentialClaim gains Save: the credential entity carries the
  claim token. Commit keeps the token on the committed claim.
- GetByUserID and GetByCredentialID (and so Update) see a token-bound
  credential only while the user's claim is committed with that token.
- Recovery of an expired pending claim that already has a credential under
  its own token rolls the claim forward to committed and refuses.

Slices: test (stale Save then restart before Commit: only the recoverer's
credential is readable, the account stays committed), then fix
(credential_store.go, interfaces.go, service.go, test doubles).
