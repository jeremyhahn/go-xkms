# Plan: #2183 Codex round-5 P1: invitation activation guarded on pending

## Objective

Invitation completion must not reactivate an account an administrator
disabled during the ceremony (Tenant Agent ruling 968, Codex round 5 on
PR #2183, pkg/xkms/servicer_invite.go:522). The direct-node activation
was an unconditional UpdateUser to active, and the pinned go-qrdb store
accepts disabled -> active.

The fix: the activation's UpdateUserRequest carries a Guard that refuses
unless the committed prior lifecycle is still pending. The conditional
commit swaps against the version the guard judged, so a disable that lands
after the ceremony's lifecycle check either is seen by the guard or makes
the commit fail with ConcurrentUpdateError. A refused activation answers
the opaque ErrInviteUnusable and leaves the invitation unredeemed.

## Slices

1. test: servicer_invite_qrdb_test.go: against the real embedded qrdb user
   store, an administrator disables the pending account after the WebAuthn
   ceremony is validated and before the activation commits (store wrapper
   at the activation call); the account stays disabled and the redemption
   fails.
2. fix: servicer_invite.go setInviteUserActive passes the pending guard;
   activateInviteUser maps a non-pending refusal to ErrInviteUnusable.

## Out of scope

The qrdb client path (s.users, no Guard over the wire), the PIV path

The other three round-4 P1s (sibling lanes) and the add-a-passkey path.

## Round 5 (ruling 968): claim-bound save

A stale claimer that saves after its claim was recovered, then crashes
before Commit, must leave no usable credential (Codex review 5975930446).

- FirstCredentialClaim gains Save: the credential entity carries the
  claim token. Commit keeps the token on the committed claim.
- GetByUserID and GetByCredentialID (and so Update) see a token-bound
  credential only while the user's claim is committed with that token.
- Recovery of an expired pending claim that already has a credential under
  its own token rolls the claim forward to committed and refuses.

Slices: test (stale Save then restart before Commit: only the recoverer's
credential is readable, the account stays committed), then fix
(credential_store.go, interfaces.go, service.go, test doubles).
