# Plan: #2319 tenant-aware passkey login (ruling 1234)

## Objective

A passkey login reaches the relying party of one tenant:

- The tenant comes from `tenant_id` or `X-Tenant-Id`, or from the request
  host's `tenant_hosts` entry; otherwise it is the system tenant.
- Account lookups are scoped to that tenant.
- An unknown or cross-tenant request gets an indistinguishable decoy, and its
  finish fails like a wrong assertion.
- The minted token carries the tenant.
- Tenant invitations enrol through their tenant's relying party.

## Slices

1. tl2319a, test(webauthn):
   - `pkg/webauthn/tenant_router_test.go` covers system/empty tenant, a known
     tenant built once, unknown tenants never built or cached, a decoy that
     resolves no account and is deterministic per tenant, the host rule,
     `tenant_hosts` validation, and qrdb `GetByID` refusing another tenant's
     account.
   - `pkg/api/rest/webauthn_tenant_login_test.go` covers the token carrying
     the tenant for email, handle and discoverable logins, the host selecting
     the tenant, and cross-tenant decoys that fail like a wrong assertion.
2. tl2319a, feat(webauthn):
   - `TenantRouter` (`pkg/webauthn/tenant_router.go`) and `Config.TenantHosts`.
   - Tenant-scoped decoy subjects, and the `GetByID` tenant check.
   - `BeginLoginRequest.TenantID` and `X-Tenant-Id`; transport `TenantID` on
     Begin/FinishAuthentication.
   - `XKMSService.SetWebAuthnTenants`/`webauthnFor` on the login and invite
     verbs, and `BuildWebAuthnTenantRouter`.
   - Wiring in xkmsd and the host REST surface, with the session sweep
     covering every routed service.
3. tl2319b (open): the gRPC and unix wire field and `xkmsctl --tenant`.
