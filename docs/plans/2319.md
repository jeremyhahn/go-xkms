# Plan: #2319 tenant-aware passkey login (ruling 1234)

## Objective

A passkey login reaches the relying party of one tenant:

- The tenant comes from `tenant_id` or `X-Tenant-Id`, or from the request
  host's `tenant_hosts` entry; otherwise it is the system tenant.
- Account lookups are scoped to that tenant.
- An unknown or cross-tenant request gets an indistinguishable decoy, and its
  finish fails like a wrong assertion.
- The minted token carries the tenant.
- Tenant invitations enrol through their tenant's relying party.
