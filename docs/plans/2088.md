# Plan: continuous-attestation (#2088)

SPEC row: `continuous-attestation` (docs/SPEC.md:81). PR #2111.

## Objective

go-xkms re-attests enrolled devices through a caller-configured
re-attestation engine, on a schedule and on demand, and publishes the
verdict. go-trusted-platform supplies the device list and the device
connections through the engine's interfaces; go-xkms holds no device
inventory (owner ruling 1024).

## Slices, in order

1. Engine and verdict store (`pkg/attestation/continuous`): scheduler,
   on-demand re-attestation, verdict store.
   Tests: `engine_test.go`, `engine_caller_test.go`, `store_test.go`.
2. Verdict store wiring (`pkg/compose`, `pkg/server`, `pkg/apimodule`).
   Tests: `pkg/compose/verdicts_test.go`, `module_host_stores_test.go`.
3. Servicer and verbs (`pkg/xkms/servicer_attestation_verdicts.go`,
   permissions, apierrors). Tests: `servicer_attestation_verdicts_test.go`.
4. Transports: REST, gRPC, MCP, QUIC, unix, embedded; SDK client.
   Tests: each `transport_attestation_verdicts_test.go`, the handler
   tests, authz golden tests, `sdk/go/attestation_verdicts_client_test.go`.
5. Finish: merge main, green CI gates (race, integration image,
   devcontainer), ready for the row's single Codex review.

## Out of scope

- Evidence fetching and the device inventory (composing product).
- Outcomes: admit, quarantine, remediate, disconnect
  (go-trusted-platform rows, enforced by its SDN).
