transports and sdk/go, and (ruling 1073 (a)) xkey appraises evidence
through the sdk/go Client (`sdk/go/transport` `AttestationAppraisalService`).

## Slice 3 unblocked: ruling 1073 (a) in three slices

`AppraiseAttestationQuote` needs a TPM quote, which neither xkey command
holds. Ruling 1073 (a) adds two appraisal verbs that take the evidence xkey
does have. They anchor in the tenant endorsement store (kinds `android-root`
and `ek-root`), return the quote verb's `AttestationResult`, and are gated by
`attestation_verifier:verify`.

- A. Done (rw2101c): `AppraiseAndroidKeyAttestation` and
  `AppraiseEKCertificate` in pkg/attestation/appraisal and pkg/xkms, the
  transport interface, and the embedded, REST, MCP and USB (not supported)
  transports. A refusal is a typed `*appraisal.EvidenceRefusedError`
  (`ErrEvidenceRefused`, class invalid-argument). gRPC, Unix and QUIC have
  compile-only not-supported stubs marked `slice B`.
  - fb020f663 test(xkms), 022e8b8ea feat(xkms)
  - 86b1066e3 test(embedded), 161ad299b feat(embedded)
  - 39a07c875 test(rest), 6c1b7d2a7 feat(rest)
  - 378ce4466 test(mcp), ac1b1fadb feat(mcp)
  - a7afe9ef8 test(usb). The not-supported stub came with the interface in
    022e8b8ea, so there is no feat(usb) commit.
- B. gRPC (proto messages and RPCs, pbconv), Unix and QUIC carry the two
  verbs; the `slice B` stubs go.
- C. Done (av1660b): sdk/go `transport` aliases the two request types and
  `ErrEvidenceRefused`; xkey `device attest` and `tpm ek show` appraise
  through the Client (slice 3 above). Open: the SPEC text names the two verbs.
